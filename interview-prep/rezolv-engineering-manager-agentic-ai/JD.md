# Job Description: Engineering Manager - Agentic AI Platform

## About Rezolv
Rezolv is the AI-native Lending OS for India's financial institutions. Our Debt Collections platform is live across 15+ lenders — including ICICI Bank, AU Small Finance Bank, Poonawalla Fincorp, Muthoot, Five Star Business Finance, Indostar, Finova, Protium, and Northern Arc Capital — delivering 20% lift in collection efficiency, 45% lower people cost, and 90% fewer customer escalations.

At the core is the Rezolv Agentic AI Platform — a production suite of voice, vision, and generative AI capabilities powering lending workflows from origination through collections, in use by India's largest banks and NBFCs today.

---

## The Role
We're hiring an **Engineering Manager** to lead and expand the engineering charter for the Agentic AI Platform. This is a player-coach role — you'll architect the next generation of our AI systems while scaling the team that owns them.

The platform you lead is already in production at India's largest banks and NBFCs, touching millions of borrowers every month — and the roadmap ahead is significantly larger than what's shipped.

**Location:** Mumbai

---

## The Platform
You'll lead engineering across four core pillars of the Agentic AI Platform — already live with customers, with substantial roadmap ahead:
*   **Full-lifecycle voice agents** — Pre-due EMI reminders, post-due collections, deep-delinquency recovery, welcome calling, lead qualification, PD calling, and more.
*   **Voice analytics** — Transcription, translation, synthesis, and actionable signal extraction across audio at scale.
*   **Multimodal document processing** — Structured extraction and comparison across documents, images, video, and audio.
*   **Multimedia generation** — For customer engagement, automation, and operational workflows.

1.  **Evolve and architect** the next generation of our production AI systems — telephony orchestration, ASR/TTS, LLM pipelines, RAG, agentic workflows, evaluation, observability, and cost control.
