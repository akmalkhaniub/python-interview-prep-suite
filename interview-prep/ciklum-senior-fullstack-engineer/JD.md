About the job
Job Description
Ciklum is looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join our team full-time in Pakistan.
We are a custom product engineering company that supports both multinational organizations and scaling startups to solve their most complex business challenges. With a global team of over 4,000 highly skilled developers, consultants, analysts and product owners, we engineer technology that redefines industries and shapes the way people live.

About The Role
As a Senior Full Stack Engineer, become a part of a cross-functional development team engineering experiences of tomorrow. This is an opportunity to work at the heart of an entrepreneurial tech team, shaping how grows and supporting the drivers of our success.
You’ll collaborate closely with other engineers, our Product team, and our Data team to design, build and deliver high-quality technology and customer experiences. While experience as a Software Engineer is essential, we’re pretty open on which languages and frameworks you’ve worked with. Our current stack includes Python, Django, Typescript, React, React Native and AWS serverless technologies (Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, API Gateway, SNS/SQS). You’ll work across frontend, backend, and platform infrastructure, with learning opportunities throughout your time to expand your skills.
Join our client, a growing company in the sustainability-focused tech space, operating in a product focused environment. Be part of a purpose driven team working to reduce waste and improve how resources are used more efficiently across supply chains. The impact goes beyond the product itself, contributing to broader environmental outcomes.

Responsibilities:
Leading on the successful delivery of complex projects and features across both the backend and frontend of our tech stack, contributing directly to key product goals by working on technical designs and building quality solutions
Increasing team velocity and efficiency through the adoption of engineering best practices
Take ownership of code quality through testing, code reviews, and documentation

Requirements:
Extensive experience with designing, developing and delivering robust engineering solutions in commercial environments (we primarily use Python, Typescript and AWS)
Solid understanding of cloud infrastructure and CI/CD (e.g., AWS, Github Actions)
Familiarity with modern software architecture and cloud design patterns
Comfortable working in high-autonomy, collaborative environments, balancing urgency with quality
Excellent problem-solving and technical debugging skills, with the ability to mitigate and solve complex issues
Strong communication and stakeholder engagement abilities – able to explain technical concepts to all audiences

Personal Skills:
Pragmatic – understand the trade-offs of software delivery
Delivery-focused – success is measured by shipping great products, not just writing code
Curious – ask questions, explore problems, and seek better solutions
Collaborative – work independently when needed, but know when to bring others in
Communicative – able to zoom in to technical detail or step back to evaluate the big picture
