# 7-Day Interview Preparation Plan: Senior AI Engineer Lead

This intensive plan is designed to align your existing AI expertise with the leadership, architecture, and specific technological requirements of Avanza Solutions.

## Day 1: Deep Dive into Avanza's Domain
- **Action:** Research Avanza Solutions' products (e.g., Symphony, Rendezvous, Ambit) and their footprint in Digital Banking, Blockchain, and Smart Cities.
- **Focus:** Understand the common pain points in these industries (security, scale, legacy integration, real-time processing).
- **Output:** Write a 1-page summary of how your past projects (e.g., agentic workflows, data pipelines) map to Avanza’s industry focus.

## Day 2: Core ML & Deep Learning Foundations
- **Action:** Review core concepts in Neural Networks and Pattern Recognition.
- **Focus:** Optimization algorithms (Adam, RMSprop), backpropagation, vanishing/exploding gradients, regularization techniques (Dropout, L1/L2), and evaluation metrics (F1, AUC-ROC, Precision/Recall trade-offs).
- **Output:** Be able to confidently explain the math and intuition behind these concepts on a whiteboard.

## Day 3: NLP & Advanced AI Frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow)
- **Action:** Brush up on state-of-the-art NLP architectures (Transformers, BERT, GPT variants).
- **Focus:** Fine-tuning strategies (PEFT, LoRA), tokenization challenges, and embedding models. Review PyTorch/TensorFlow best practices, custom training loops, and distributed data parallel (DDP).
- **Output:** Prepare a 5-minute technical monologue on a complex NLP problem you solved, focusing on framework-specific implementations.

## Day 4: System Architecture & MLOps
- **Action:** Practice designing scalable AI systems.
- **Focus:** Real-time vs. Batch inference, Feature Stores, Model Registries (MLflow), Model Deployment (Triton, ONNX, Ray Serve), and monitoring for data drift.
- **Output:** Draw an end-to-end architecture diagram for a real-time banking fraud detection system or a smart city traffic optimization pipeline.

## Day 5: Software Engineering & Production Code
- **Action:** Review advanced software development processes in the context of ML.
- **Focus:** CI/CD for ML, testing ML systems (unit tests, integration tests, data validation with Great Expectations), containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), and API design (FastAPI/gRPC).
- **Output:** Ensure you can discuss how you maintain code quality and manage technical debt in data-heavy projects.

## Day 6: Leadership, Mentorship & Behavioral Prep
- **Action:** Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to prepare stories.
- **Focus:** 
  - Resolving a technical dispute.
  - Mentoring a junior engineer who was struggling.
  - Leading a project that was falling behind schedule.
  - Communicating complex AI limitations to non-technical stakeholders (Product Managers).
- **Output:** Write down 5 versatile STAR stories that highlight your leadership and cross-functional collaboration.

## Day 7: Mock Interview & Review
- **Action:** Conduct a mock interview focusing on system design and leadership.
- **Focus:** Presenting your portfolio. Rehearse the "Tell me about yourself" pitch, heavily leaning into your experience as an AI Lead.
- **Output:** Rest, review your notes, and ensure your mindset is positioned as a strategic technical leader.
