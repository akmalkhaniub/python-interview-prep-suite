# Senior AI Engineer Lead at Avanza Solutions

## About the job
### Company Description
Avanza Solutions, established in 2000, is a global leader in driving digital transformation through innovative and advanced digital platforms and services. Specializing in diverse technologies like Digital Banking, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, and Smart City applications, Avanza delivers tailored solutions to empower enterprises. The company's people-centric approach has enabled successful innovation and automation for industries such as Banking, Finance, Telecommunications, Insurance, Pharmaceuticals, and Government. Avanza’s expertise in cutting-edge solutions has consistently transformed the operational, financial, and functional landscapes of organizations worldwide.

### Role Description
This is a full-time onsite role for a Senior AI Engineer Lead at Avanza Solutions. The Senior AI Engineer Lead will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying advanced artificial intelligence solutions to solve complex business problems. Day-to-day tasks include leading AI initiatives, developing algorithms, building and optimizing machine learning models, and mentoring junior team members. Collaboration with cross-functional teams, including product managers and software developers, will be essential to ensure streamlined implementation of AI-driven strategies.

### Qualifications
- Strong knowledge and experience in Neural Networks and Pattern Recognition
- Proficiency in Computer Science principles and advanced Software Development processes
- Proven expertise in developing and working with Natural Language Processing (NLP) solutions
- Demonstrated leadership and mentoring abilities in managing AI engineering teams
- Experience with AI frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or similar tools
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to solve complex data-driven challenges
- Master’s or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or a related field preferred
- Previous work experience in Financial, Telecommunication, or related technology-driven industries is a plus
