# Senior App & Frontend Developer (Smart Working)

## About the Role
We are looking for a Senior App & Frontend Developer to take a leading role in the development of modern web and mobile applications. This is a senior, hands-on engineering position focused primarily on frontend development, where you will help shape architecture, drive UI quality, and support the wider engineering team in delivering scalable, maintainable applications.

## Responsibilities
*   Lead frontend and app development across web and mobile platforms
*   Design, build, and maintain scalable frontend applications using React and modern frameworks
*   Develop hybrid mobile applications using Ionic and Capacitor
*   Define and implement frontend architecture, standards, and best practices
*   Ensure responsive, performant, and accessible UI across devices and platforms
*   Support and mentor other engineers within the team
*   Evaluate and introduce modern frontend technologies and AI tools to improve development efficiency
*   Drive technical decisions and contribute to project planning and delivery

## Requirements
*   **React:** 10+ years of experience for building production-grade applications
*   **Angular:** 5+ years of experience (including version 15+)
*   **Hybrid Mobile:** Strong experience with Ionic (7+) and Capacitor
*   **AI Tools:** Experience using AI tools to support software development workflows
*   **Leadership:** Proven experience leading frontend development efforts or technical delivery
*   **Soft Skills:** Ability to take ownership in a small team environment; strong communication skills.

## Nice to Have
*   Experience leading frontend architecture in small or mid-sized teams
*   Exposure to cross-platform mobile development challenges
*   Familiarity with modern design systems and UI component libraries
*   Strong interest in AI-assisted development and modern engineering workflows
