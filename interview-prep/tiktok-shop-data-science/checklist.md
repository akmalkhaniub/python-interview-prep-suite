# Interview Prep Checklist: Data Scientist (TikTok Shop)

## Phase 1: Growth & Stats (Days 1-2)
- [ ] **A/B Testing Masterclass:** Review Sample Size, Power, Interference, and Multiple Testing corrections (Bonferroni).
- [ ] **User Metrics:** Master the definitions of DAU, MAU, CR, GMV, and ROI in an e-commerce context.
- [ ] **Funnel Analysis:** Practice whiteboarding the TikTok Shop user journey.

## Phase 2: Risk & Machine Learning (Days 3-4)
- [ ] **Risk Modeling:** Review Anomaly Detection and Binary Classification (XGBoost/LightGBM).
- [ ] **Causal Inference:** Learn the basics of "Uplift Modeling" for promotion ROI.
- [ ] **Feature Engineering:** Brainstorm 10 features that predict e-commerce purchase intent from video behavior.

## Phase 3: Big Data & SQL (Days 5-6)
- [ ] **SQL Hard Level:** Practice Window Functions, Recursive CTEs, and handling large joins.
- [ ] **Spark Basics:** Understand Map-Reduce, RDD vs. Dataframe, and Shuffle operations.
- [ ] **Hive Partitioning:** Review how to optimize queries on terabyte-scale tables.

## Phase 4: Product Sense & Culture (Day 7)
- [ ] **TikTok Shop Product Research:** Use the app to understand the "Live E-commerce" and "Short Video Shop" flows.
- [ ] **"Always Day 1" Mindset:** Prepare stories that demonstrate resilience and rapid iteration.
- [ ] **Business Case Study:** Practice a "New Market Entry" or "Subsidy Optimization" case study.

---
**Key Terms to Master:**
- **GMV:** Gross Merchandise Volume.
- **Uplift Modeling:** Predicting incremental impact.
- **Data Skew:** Imbalance in data distribution.
- **Network Effects:** How users impact each other in a social graph.
