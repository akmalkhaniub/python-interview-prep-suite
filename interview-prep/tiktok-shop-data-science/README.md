# Data Science & Analytics - TikTok Shop

This module focuses on the Data Science role at TikTok Shop, targeting C-end user growth, traffic conversion, user experience optimization, and risk control. The role combines deep technical skills in Big Data and ML with high business acumen in the global e-commerce sector.

## 📁 Module Contents

1.  **[JD Analysis](./jd_analysis.md)**: Breakdown of TikTok Shop's unique "Content E-commerce" model, growth focus, and risk control requirements.
2.  **[Technical Study Guide](./study_guide.md)**: Deep dives into A/B testing, ROI optimization, Causal Inference, and the Hadoop/Spark ecosystem.
3.  **[Preparation Checklist](./checklist.md)**: A 7-day roadmap focusing on stats, big data engineering, and TikTok's "Always Day 1" culture.

## 🛠️ Key Technologies
- **Languages:** SQL (Expert), Python, R.
- **Big Data:** Hadoop, Spark, Hive.
- **Stats & ML:** A/B Testing, Causal Inference, Uplift Modeling, Deep Learning.
- **Focus:** Traffic conversion, ROI optimization, Risk modeling.

## 🛍️ Strategic Focus: Content E-commerce
- Bridging the gap between short-video engagement and physical product purchase.
- Optimizing user subsidies to maximize platform growth and GMV.
- Ensuring platform safety through intelligent risk control rules.
