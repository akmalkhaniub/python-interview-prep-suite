## Role Focus
- **Stack:** React, React Native, Node.js/Python, PostgreSQL/MongoDB, Docker, AWS/GCP.
- **Experience:** 2-3 years (Strong mid-level expectations).
- **Environment:** Remote (Permanent), US Team Collaboration.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **Full-Stack versatility**—show that you are equally comfortable in React as you are in Node or Python.
- Emphasize your **Docker** and **Cloud** knowledge; US startups value engineers who can manage their own deployments.
- Be prepared for **System Design** questions that test your ability to build scalable features.
- Highlight your **communication skills** for a remote, distributed environment.
