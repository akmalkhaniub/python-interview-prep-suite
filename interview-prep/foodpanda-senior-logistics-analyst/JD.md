About the job
foodpanda: Senior Data Analyst (Logistics Analytics)

As a Senior Data Analyst (Logistics Analytics), you will leverage your expertise to directly impact the growth of our 16 markets by delivering actionable insights and advanced analytics solutions. This is a high-visibility position that plays a pivotal role in data-driven decision-making.

Key Responsibilities:
Own a logistics problem domain end-to-end (cost, efficiency, service performance).
Apply advanced analytical methods (simulation, optimization, statistical modeling) to solve fleet sizing, routing, staffing, and demand planning.
Determine causal impact in operational environments using DiD, synthetic controls, and cluster/switchback experiments.
Build and scale data products using modern data stacks and agentic AI workflows.
Drive exploratory and root cause analyses.
Collaborate cross-functionally on data modeling and metric definitions.
Communicate complex analyses into actionable recommendations for business stakeholders.
Mentor junior analysts.

Qualifications:
Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field (Statistics, OR, Math, CS, Engineering).
5–8 years of experience in analytics or data science.
Proven experience in logistics, supply chain, or operations analytics.
Strong technical proficiency in SQL and Python.
Experience with Google Cloud ecosystem (BigQuery, Vertex AI).
Experience in causal inference and experimentation (A/B, cluster, switchback, DiD, synthetic controls).

What we offer:
A dynamic and challenging work environment.
Responsibility from day one in a fast growing and global company.
Free food, health and dental insurance, and L&D opportunities!
