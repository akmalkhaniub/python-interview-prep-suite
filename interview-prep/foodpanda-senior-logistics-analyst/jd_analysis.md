# Job Description Analysis: Senior Data Analyst (Logistics) - foodpanda

## Role Overview
**Company:** foodpanda (Delivery Hero Group)
**Position:** Senior Data Analyst (Logistics Analytics)
**Experience:** 5-8 years in Data Science or Analytics.
**Core Mission:** Driving operational efficiency across 16 markets through advanced logistics optimization, causal inference, and automated analytics tools.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Logistics & Operations Optimization
- **Fleet Management:** Fleet sizing, staffing, and routing optimization.
- **Planning:** Demand planning and service performance improvement.
- **Methods:** Simulation, mathematical optimization, and statistical modeling.

### 2. Causal Inference & Experimentation
- **Operational Experiments:** Designing and analyzing **Cluster** and **Switchback** experiments.
- **Quasi-Experiments:** Applying **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** and **Synthetic Control** methods to measure impact where randomization is difficult.

### 3. Modern Data Stack & AI
- **GCP Ecosystem:** Expertise in **BigQuery** and Vertex AI.
- **Agentic AI:** Building AI workflows to enable "Self-serve" analytics and deep dives.
- **Data Products:** Scaling analytical tools and dashboards for business leads.

### 4. Leadership & Communication
- **Thought Partnership:** Challenging assumptions and co-developing solutions with domain leads.
- **Mentorship:** Elevating the skills of junior analysts.
- **Clarity:** Translating complex technical results into clear, actionable recommendations.

## Key Interview Themes to Prepare

1.  **Logistics Problem Solving:** Walk through how you would optimize "Fleet Sizing" for a new city in Southeast Asia.
2.  **Experimental Design (Switchback):** Why use a Switchback experiment instead of a standard A/B test in a delivery marketplace? (Interference/Network Effects).
3.  **Causal Impact Measurement:** Describing a time you used **DiD** or **Synthetic Controls** to measure the impact of a logistics change (e.g., a new routing algorithm).
4.  **Big Data with SQL/Python:** How do you optimize a query that scans billions of delivery rows in BigQuery to ensure fast insights?
5.  **Agentic AI for Analytics:** Discussing how LLMs or AI agents can speed up the "Time-to-Insight" for operational managers.
6.  **Simulation & Root Cause:** Building a simulation model to identify the root cause of "High Delivery Latency" during peak hours.

## Technical "Must-Haves" for the Interview
- Strong **SQL** (BigQuery) and **Python** skills.
- Deep understanding of **Causal Inference** methods.
- Proven experience in **Logistics/Supply Chain** analytics.
- Ability to structure ambiguous business problems into analytical frameworks.
