# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Senior Logistics Analyst (foodpanda)

## Day 1: Logistics Fundamentals & Fleet Optimization
- [ ] Review **Fleet Sizing** and **Staffing** optimization strategies.
- [ ] Research **Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP)** basics and heuristics.
- [ ] Study **Uber's H3 Hexagonal Indexing** for spatial analytics.

## Day 2: Advanced SQL (BigQuery) & Big Data
- [ ] Review **Window Functions** for analyzing delivery sequences (LAG, LEAD, RANK).
- [ ] Practice **BigQuery optimization** techniques (Partitioning, Clustering).
- [ ] Study **BigQuery GIS** functions for geospatial analysis.

## Day 3: Causal Inference & Quasi-Experiments
- [ ] Review **Difference-in-Differences (DiD)** and its Parallel Trends assumption.
- [ ] Study **Synthetic Control Method** and its implementation in Python.
- [ ] Research **Propensity Score Matching** for observational data.

## Day 4: Experimental Design (Switchback)
- [ ] Review **Switchback Testing** architecture and why it's used in logistics.
- [ ] Study how to handle **Network Interference** in experimental design.
- [ ] Practice calculating **Sample Sizes** for cluster-based experiments.

## Day 5: Simulation & Optimization in Python
- [ ] Review **SimPy** or similar libraries for discrete event simulation.
- [ ] Practice building a simple **Fleet Sizing simulation** script.
- [ ] Study **SciPy.optimize** for resource allocation problems.

## Day 6: Root Cause Analysis & Metrics
- [ ] Practice **Decomposing Delivery Time** into sub-metrics.
- [ ] Prepare stories on identifying **Logistics Bottlenecks** using data.
- [ ] Study **Anomaly Detection** patterns in operational time-series data.

## Day 7: Pitch & Stakeholder Communication
- [ ] Finalize your **Logistics success stories**: Problems solved, impact delivered.
- [ ] Prepare your "Thought Partner" pitch: How do you challenge business assumptions?
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain the **Causal Impact** of a routing change to a non-technical manager.
