## Role Focus
- **Stack:** SQL, Python, GCP (BigQuery, Vertex AI).
- **Concepts:** Causal Inference (DiD, Synthetic Controls), Optimization, Simulation, Switchback Testing.
- **Domain:** Logistics & Operations (Fleet sizing, Routing, Demand Planning).

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **Causal Inference** aspect—it's a significant part of the JD and critical for measuring impact in complex logistics.
- Be prepared for **Big Data technicals**; foodpanda operates in 16 markets and values efficient SQL/Python performance.
- Highlight your **Logistics domain experience**; they are looking for a track record in fleet sizing or demand planning.
- Emphasize your **Problem Structuring** skills; they value analysts who can frame ambiguous problems end-to-end.
- Mention your familiarity with **Agentic AI**; it's a "modern analytics" trend they are explicitly looking for.
