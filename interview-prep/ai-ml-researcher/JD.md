# AI/ML Researcher

## Role Overview
We are hiring an AI/ML Researcher to advance the frontier of how our models work. The role combines scientific rigor with engineering pragmatism — running experiments, publishing findings, and shaping the research agenda.

**Location:** Remote  
**Compensation:** $140,000 – $260,000 + Benefits

---

## Key Responsibilities
- Design and run experiments that advance model capabilities or alignment.
- Implement, train, and evaluate novel architectures and methods.
- Develop benchmarks and evaluation frameworks.
- Document findings clearly through internal write-ups, papers, or presentations.
- Collaborate with engineering on transitioning research into production.
- Contribute to the broader research roadmap.

---

## Required Skills and Qualifications
- **PhD in CS, ML, or related field**, or equivalent industry research experience.
- Deep expertise in **modern deep learning**, including transformers and large-scale training.
- Strong **PyTorch** (or equivalent) skills.
- Track record of **publications, open-source work**, or shipped research projects.
- Excellent **written communication**, including paper-quality technical writing.
- Curiosity and willingness to dig into hard, ambiguous problems.

---

## What You'll Bring
- Curiosity about how models behave and why they fail.
- Rigor in following guidelines and flagging ambiguity early.
- Strong written communication, including ability to document findings clearly.
- Comfort working asynchronously across time zones.

---

## Tech Stack (Implicit & Expected)
- **Deep Learning Frameworks:** PyTorch, JAX
- **Architectures:** Transformers (LLMs, ViT), Diffusion Models
- **Training:** FSDP, DeepSpeed, Megatron-LM
- **Alignment:** RLHF, DPO, PPO
- **Analysis:** Jupyter, TensorBoard, WandB
