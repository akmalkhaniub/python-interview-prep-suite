# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: AI Engineer (Treasury Cube)

## Day 1: Azure AI Foundations
- [ ] Review Azure OpenAI Service: Model deployment, GPT-4o capabilities, and rate limits.
- [ ] Study Azure AI Foundry: Creating projects and using the Prompt Flow designer.
- [ ] Hands-on: Use the Azure OpenAI Playground to test function calling with sample financial schemas.

## Day 2: Advanced Retrieval (RAG)
- [ ] Review Azure AI Search: Vector search, Hybrid search, and Semantic Ranker.
- [ ] Practice "Chunking" strategies for financial tables (PDF parsing).
- [ ] Research "Document Intelligence" prebuilt and custom neural models.

## Day 3: FinTech & Banking Standards
- [ ] Read the basics of **ISO 20022** (CAMT and PAIN messages).
- [ ] Understand **Cash Positioning** and **FX Exposure** concepts.
- [ ] Research how bank statements (MT940/CAMT.053) are processed in modern TMS systems.

## Day 4: Agentic Workflows & Orchestration
- [ ] Review **Semantic Kernel** (if C#) or **LangChain** (if Python).
- [ ] Study **Azure Durable Functions** for stateful, long-running AI workflows.
- [ ] Design a mock "Payment Investigation" agent workflow on paper.

## Day 5: Trust, Security, & Multi-tenancy
- [ ] Study **Data Isolation** patterns in Azure (Row-Level Security).
- [ ] Review **Azure Content Safety** for filtering sensitive financial data.
- [ ] Formulate your answer to: "How do you earn a CFO's trust with an AI assistant?"

## Day 6: MLOps & Evaluation
- [ ] Review **Azure AI Evaluation SDK**: Faithfulness, Relevance, and Groundedness.
- [ ] Research token cost tracking strategies in a multi-tenant SaaS.
- [ ] Practice explaining "Prompt Regression Testing."

## Day 7: Strategic Thinking & Behavioral
- [ ] Prepare "Behavioral" stories: Times you took ownership, built from scratch, or solved a complex hallucination issue.
- [ ] Final review of the **Treasury Cube** mission and the "CFO Assistant" deep-dive.
- [ ] Polish your GitHub/Portfolio for your application email.
