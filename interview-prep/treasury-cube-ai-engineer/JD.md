About the job
About Treasury Cube
Treasury Cube is a modern Treasury Management System (TMS) helping corporate treasury teams take control of cash, payments, bank connectivity, in-house banking, and FX exposure. We're building a platform that turns the daily grind of treasury operations, bank statement processing, cash positioning, intercompany settlements, payment workflows into something faster, smarter, and decision ready.

We're now embedding AI deeply into the platform to give treasurers the kind of insight, automation, and conversational access that legacy TMS vendors simply can't deliver. This is where you come in.

The Role
We're looking for an AI Engineer to lead the design and delivery of AI capabilities across Treasury Cube. You'll own the AI engineering stack end-to-end from model orchestration on Azure AI, to retrieval pipelines over treasury data, to the production systems that put insights and automation in front of CFOs and treasurers every day.

This is a hands-on, build-from-the-ground-up role. You'll shape architecture, set patterns, and ship features customers actually use within weeks, not quarters.

What You'll Build
AI Insights Engine: Generate explanatory commentary and recommendations on cash positions, FX exposures, payment anomalies, forecast variances, and intercompany flows. Think: turning a 12-bank, 8-currency cash dashboard into a one-paragraph "here's what changed and why" briefing.
AI Chatbox / Conversational Treasury Assistant: A natural-language interface where users can ask "what's my USD position next Friday?" or "show me payments held up in approval over $500K" and get accurate, grounded answers backed by the underlying treasury data.
AI-Based Task & Notification Automation: Intelligent routing, prioritization, and summarization of alerts (covenant breaches, sweep failures, payment exceptions, hedge effectiveness drift). Auto-generated tasks with context, suggested actions, and assignment logic.
Monitoring & Observability for AI Systems: Token usage, latency, hallucination detection, prompt regression, cost-per-customer tracking, and human-in-the-loop feedback pipelines. If we can't measure it, we won't trust it in front of treasurers.
