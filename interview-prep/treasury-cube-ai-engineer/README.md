## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search, Azure AI Document Intelligence, Python/C#.
- **Mission:** Embedding AI into the "daily grind" of treasury operations (cash, payments, FX).
- **Key Challenge:** Earning the trust of a CFO by ensuring grounded, accurate, and auditable AI insights.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **Azure AI** experience (specifically Search and OpenAI Service).
- Be prepared to discuss **accuracy and grounding**—CFOs have zero tolerance for data hallucinations.
- Highlight any experience with **structured financial data** (ISO 20022) or **multi-tenant security**.
