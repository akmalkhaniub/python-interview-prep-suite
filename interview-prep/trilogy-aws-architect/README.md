## Role Focus
- **Stack:** AWS (Expert), Python (Boto3), Terraform, Ansible, MySQL/Postgres/Oracle.
- **Mission:** Driving 99.9% uptime across 50+ acquired SaaS products.
- **Key Skill:** 5-Whys Root Cause Analysis and "AI-assisted" system discovery.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **independent problem-solving ability**—Trilogy values engineers who build without hand-holding.
- Be prepared for **real-time troubleshooting assessments**; they will observe how you think and act under pressure.
- Emphasize your **Python scripting** for AWS; this is a "production-grade" requirement, not just basic automation.
- Mention your comfort with the **Crossover/Independent Contractor** model early in the conversation.
