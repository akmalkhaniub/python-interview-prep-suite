# Job Description Analysis: Senior Data Scientist - easypaisa digital bank

## Role Overview
**Company:** easypaisa (Telenor Microfinance Bank / Ant Group)
**Position:** Senior Data Scientist (ML & Gen AI)
**Experience:** 5+ years in Data Science.
**Core Mission:** Driving the digital bank's evolution through advanced predictive modeling for credit risk, customer segmentation, and the integration of GenAI/Agentic AI for intelligent automation.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Machine Learning & Statistical Modeling
- **Segmentation:** Clustering customers by behavior, payment patterns, and lifestyle.
- **Churn & Propensity:** Predicting app churn and the likelihood of adopting new digital products.
- **Market Basket Analysis:** Using Association Rule Mining to develop product affinity scores.

### 2. Credit Risk & Fintech Analytics
- **Scoring:** Developing robust credit scoring models for a digital-first audience.
- **Risk Modeling:** Building **Probability of Default (PD)** models and optimizing collection strategies.
- **Drill-downs:** Using complex SQL to perform deep-dive investigative analytics on transactional data.

### 3. Generative & Agentic AI
- **Automation:** Implementing GenAI to automate internal processes and customer interactions.
- **Agentic Frameworks:** Designing autonomous agents that can navigate banking data and perform reasoning tasks.
- **Experience:** Requirement of at least 2 years in this specific domain.

### 4. MLOps & Governance
- **Deployment:** Building scalable pipelines for model deployment and monitoring.
- **Governance:** Ensuring data science projects meet banking regulatory and security standards.

## Key Interview Themes to Prepare

1.  **Credit Scoring for the Unbanked:** How do you build a credit model for users with little traditional financial history? (Focus on behavioral/telco data).
2.  **Probability of Default (PD) Modeling:** Walk through the lifecycle of building and validating a PD model in a high-velocity digital bank.
3.  **Agentic AI in Banking:** Describe a use case for an autonomous AI agent within easypaisa (e.g., automated fraud investigation or personalized financial advice).
4.  **Handling Transactional Big Data:** How do you optimize SQL queries and Python workflows for a user base of millions?
5.  **Association Rule Mining:** Explain how you would use product affinity scores to drive a "cross-sell" campaign in the easypaisa app.
6.  **The "Bridge" Role:** How do you translate a "Business Goal" (e.g., "Reduce loan defaults by 5%") into a technical Data Science project?

## Technical "Must-Haves" for the Interview
- 5+ years of **MLE/DS** experience.
- Strong **Python** and **SQL** skills.
- Hands-on experience with **GenAI/Agentic AI** frameworks (LangChain, AutoGen).
- Knowledge of **Credit Risk** and **MLOps**.
