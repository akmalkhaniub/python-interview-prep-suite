About the job
easypaisa digital bank: Senior Data Scientist (ML & Gen AI)

easypaisa is Pakistan’s first digital bank and has advanced Pakistan’s digital finance landscape. The role is responsible for driving advanced data science initiatives, leveraging machine learning and Generative AI to deliver actionable insights, predictive models, and intelligent automation.

Key Responsibilities:
Contribute to the development, assessment, automation and governance of data science projects.
Develop statistical models: predictive, clustering & segmentation (behaviour, payment, lifestyle etc.), digital (app propensity, app churn), association rule mining.
Credit and associated default risk establishment (credit scoring, probability of default modelling, collections optimization).
Design and development of model deployment pipelines (MLOps).
At least 2 years working experience with Gen AI and Agentic AI.
Perform ad hoc drill downs and investigative analytics using complex SQL.
Work with various departments to provide insights and act as a bridge between commercial users and technology.

Requirements:
Education: BS(CS/Mathematics/Engineering/Statistics), Masters preferred.
Experience: 5+ years’ experience working in Data Science (ML & Gen AI).
Behavioral Skills: Communication, Negotiation, Stakeholder management.
Competencies: SQL, Python, Gen AI and Agentic AI frameworks.
