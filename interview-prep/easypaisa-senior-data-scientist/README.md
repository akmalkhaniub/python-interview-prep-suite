## Role Focus
- **Stack:** SQL, Python, LangChain, CrewAI, MLflow.
- **Concepts:** Credit Scoring, PD Models, Clustering, Association Rule Mining, Agentic AI, RAG.
- **Domain:** Digital Banking & Fintech (Risk, Engagement, Automation).

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **Credit Risk and PD Modeling** aspect—it's a core function of the digital bank.
- Be prepared for **Agentic AI technicals**; they explicitly ask for 2+ years of experience in this domain.
- Highlight your **SQL proficiency**; they mention "complex SQL queries" as a day-to-day activity.
- Emphasize your ability to **Bridge the Gap** between technology and commercial users.
- Mention your familiarity with **Alternative Data** for credit scoring in the Pakistani market.
