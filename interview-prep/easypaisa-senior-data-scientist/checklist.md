# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Senior Data Scientist (easypaisa)

## Day 1: Fintech & Credit Risk Basics
- [ ] Review **Probability of Default (PD)** modeling and scorecard development.
- [ ] Study **Credit Scoring** metrics (Gini, KS, AUC-ROC).
- [ ] Research **Alternative Data** for credit scoring in emerging markets (e.g., Telco data).

## Day 2: Segmentation & Product Affinity
- [ ] Review **K-Means** and **Hierarchical Clustering** for behavioral segmentation.
- [ ] Practice **Association Rule Mining** (Apriori/FP-Growth) in Python.
- [ ] Study **Market Basket Analysis** use cases for cross-selling in banking apps.

## Day 3: GenAI & Agentic AI Frameworks
- [ ] Review **LangChain** and **CrewAI** for building autonomous agents.
- [ ] Practice building a **RAG pipeline** with FAISS or Pinecone.
- [ ] Study **Prompt Engineering** techniques for financial reasoning.

## Day 4: Advanced SQL & Data Drill-downs
- [ ] Review **Window Functions** for analyzing transactional logs.
- [ ] Practice **Complex Aggregations** (e.g., LTV, Churn probability at a cohort level).
- [ ] Study **Query Optimization** for high-volume banking data.

## Day 5: MLOps & Model Governance
- [ ] Review **MLOps** basics (CI/CD for ML, model monitoring).
- [ ] Study **Concept Drift** detection and retraining strategies.
- [ ] Research **Banking Regulations** (SBP) related to AI and data privacy.

## Day 6: Churn & Propensity Modeling
- [ ] Review **Churn Prediction** using survival analysis or classification.
- [ ] Practice building a **Product Propensity** model (e.g., Logistic Regression/XGBoost).
- [ ] Study **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** calculation methods.

## Day 7: Pitch & Stakeholder Management
- [ ] Finalize your **Fintech success stories**: Impact on defaults or engagement.
- [ ] Prepare your "Bridge" pitch: How do you translate business needs into AI?
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain the **Risk of AI Bias** in credit decisions to a business head.
