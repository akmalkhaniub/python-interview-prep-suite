# Job Description Analysis: Senior Data Scientist - DHL Express

## Role Overview
**Company:** DHL Express (Global Head Office)
**Position:** Senior Data Scientist (IC + People Lead)
**Location:** Singapore
**Focus:** ML, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Logistics Optimization, Technical Leadership.
**Level:** 7+ years experience.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Advanced AI & Machine Learning
- **Generative AI:** LLMs, RAG, Prompt Design, and AI-assisted automation.
- **Agentic AI:** Designing autonomous or semi-autonomous agent workflows with defined guardrails.
- **Classical ML:** Predictive modelling, statistical modelling for logistics/sales/pricing.

### 2. Technical Leadership & Mentorship
- **People Lead:** Coaching and mentoring data scientists within product teams.
- **Project Ownership:** Managing multiple initiatives, POCs, and feasibility assessments.
- **Strategy:** Defining AI strategies aligned with global business needs.

### 3. Solution Lifecycle & Operations
- **Deployment:** Leading the validation and deployment of models into production.
- **Operations:** MLOps, DataOps, and AI lifecycle management.
- **Monitoring:** Performance tracking, explainability, bias detection, and risk management.

### 4. Domain Knowledge: Logistics
- **Business Areas:** Global logistics operations, pricing, customer management, and finance.
- **Communication:** Translating complex AI concepts for senior management and non-technical stakeholders.

## Key Requirements
- **Experience:** 7+ years in data science/analytics with production-level exposure.
- **Skills:** Python, SQL, Agile delivery, Data visualization.
- **Mindset:** Solution-oriented, structured, and strong business awareness.

## Interview Themes to Prepare
1.  **Agentic Workflows in Logistics:** Designing an agent-based system to automate exception handling in global shipping routes.
2.  **Scalable GenAI:** Implementing a RAG system that scales across DHL's global head office while ensuring data governance.
3.  **Leadership & Coaching:** How to mentor junior data scientists on adopting emerging AI patterns (like Agents) while maintaining quality standards.
4.  **Business Impact (Pricing/Sales):** Using ML to optimize dynamic pricing or lead scoring for DHL's global sales team.
5.  **Governance & Risk:** Ensuring explainability and bias control in models that impact customer-facing logistics services.
