# Interview Prep Checklist: Senior Data Scientist (DHL)

## Phase 1: Domain & Business Strategy (Days 1-2)
- [ ] **Logistics Deep Dive:** Research DHL Express's core operations (Last-mile, Customs, Global Hubs).
- [ ] **KPI Alignment:** Understand how AI can impact ROX (Return on Experience) and operational efficiency in logistics.
- [ ] **Business Translation:** Practice explaining RAG and Agents to a "Head of Pricing" or "Logistics Director."

## Phase 2: Agentic AI & GenAI (Days 3-4)
- [ ] **Master LangGraph/Workflow patterns:** Focus on stateful agents and human-in-the-loop patterns.
- [ ] **Guardrails Implementation:** Research NeMo Guardrails or LlamaGuard for enterprise safety.
- [ ] **Document Processing:** Review OCR + LLM workflows for shipping/customs documentation.

## Phase 3: Senior Leadership & MLOps (Days 5-6)
- [ ] **Mentorship Frameworks:** Reflect on your experience coaching junior developers/scientists.
- [ ] **MLOps for Logistics:** Design a high-level architecture for a global model deployment pipeline (CI/CD/CT).
- [ ] **Governance & Ethics:** Review DHL's potential concerns: data privacy, bias in delivery estimates, and explainability.

## Phase 4: Final Preparation (Day 7)
- [ ] **STAR Stories (Senior Focus):** Prepare stories on project ownership, cross-functional collaboration, and overcoming technical debt.
- [ ] **Technical POC Design:** Be ready to whiteboard a "Proof of Concept" for a specific DHL problem (e.g., automated email triage for customer service).
- [ ] **Review Singapore Tech Context:** Mention local tech ecosystems or regulations if relevant.

---
**Key Terminology to Master:**
- **Agentic Workflows:** Multi-step AI processes with reasoning.
- **DataOps/MLOps:** Industrializing the AI lifecycle.
- **Supply Chain Analytics:** Terms like Lead Time, Throughput, and Demand Elasticity.
