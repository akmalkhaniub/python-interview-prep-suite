About the job
Role Overview
We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Data Scientist specializing in Computer Vision to lead the design, development, and deployment of advanced vision-based AI systems. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in traditional computer vision, feature engineering, and modern deep learning techniques, along with hands-on experience in building Agentic AI systems for intelligent decision-making.

Key Responsibilities:
Design and develop end-to-end computer vision pipelines for real-world applications.
Apply and optimize traditional computer vision algorithms: Image processing, feature extraction, geometric transformations, tracking, and calibration.
Perform advanced feature engineering: Extract meaningful visual features (texture, shape, color, spatial features).
Build and deploy deep learning models for: Object Detection, Instance and Semantic Segmentation.
Develop and integrate Agentic AI systems that: Enable autonomous reasoning and decision-making, orchestrate multi-step workflows, integrate with external tools and APIs.
Optimize models for performance, scalability, and edge deployment.
Lead model evaluation, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

Required Skills & Qualifications:
Experience: 6–8 Years (B.Tech), 4-6 Years (M.Tech) in Data Science / Computer Vision / AI roles.
Strong expertise in traditional computer vision: Image filtering, edge detection, morphology, feature matching, object tracking.
Strong experience in feature engineering for vision tasks.
Deep expertise in deep learning for vision: Object Detection, Semantic Segmentation, and Instance Segmentation.
Hands-on experience with: PyTorch / TensorFlow / OpenCV / Onnx.
Strong programming skills in Python.

Agentic AI Expertise:
Experience in designing Agent-based AI systems using LLMs.
Familiarity with: Tool usage, planning, memory, and orchestration frameworks (LangChain, AutoGen).
Ability to combine vision models with LLMs for intelligent workflows.

Preferred Qualifications:
Experience with Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and multimodal systems.
Knowledge of MLOps / model lifecycle management (MLflow, Azure ML, etc.).
Experience in real-time or edge AI systems.

Requirements added by the job poster:
3+ years of work experience with Computer Vision.
1+ years of work experience with Agentic AI Development.
