## Role Focus
- **Stack:** OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLO, LangChain, AutoGen, ONNX.
- **Concepts:** Instance Segmentation, Agentic Reasonings, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), MLOps.
- **Seniority:** 6-8 years (B.Tech) / 4-6 years (M.Tech) with leadership and mentoring expectations.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **integration of Vision and LLMs**—this is the "Agentic AI" part that makes this role unique.
- Be prepared for a **deep dive into traditional CV** (OpenCV); they explicitly mention feature matching, tracking, and calibration.
- Highlight your **MLOps and optimization** skills; production-grade solutions require more than just a working model.
- Prepare to discuss **architectural leadership**: How do you lead a team of AI engineers to build a complex, multimodal product?
