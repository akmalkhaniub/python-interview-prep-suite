# Job Description Analysis: Senior Data Scientist (CV + Agentic AI)

## Role Overview
**Position:** Senior Data Scientist
**Focus:** Computer Vision (Traditional + Deep Learning) & Agentic AI (LLM Orchestration).
**Experience:** 6-8 years (B.Tech) / 4-6 years (M.Tech).
**Core Mission:** Leading the design and deployment of vision-based AI systems that can reason and make decisions autonomously.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Computer Vision (Traditional & Deep Learning)
- **Traditional CV:** Expertise in OpenCV, image filtering, edge detection, geometric transformations, and feature matching.
- **Deep Learning:** Production-grade skills in Object Detection, Semantic/Instance Segmentation (PyTorch/TensorFlow).
- **Optimization:** Edge deployment using ONNX and model quantization.

### 2. Feature Engineering & Embeddings
- **Handcrafted Features:** Texture, shape, color, and spatial features.
- **Modern Features:** Combining handcrafted features with deep learning embeddings for robust representation.

### 3. Agentic AI & Multimodal Systems
- **LLM Orchestration:** Designing autonomous agents using LangChain or AutoGen.
- **Workflow Automation:** Multi-step reasoning, tool usage, and memory management.
- **VLMs:** Integrating Vision-Language Models for intelligent, multimodal workflows.

### 4. Engineering & Leadership
- **MLOps:** Managing the model lifecycle using MLflow or Azure ML.
- **Architecture:** Leading architectural decisions and mentoring junior members.
- **Domain:** Experience in Automotive, Insurance, Retail, or Manufacturing is a plus.

## Key Interview Themes to Prepare

1.  **Bridging Traditional CV and DL:** When would you use a traditional edge detector vs. a deep learning model? How do you combine them?
2.  **Advanced Segmentation & Detection:** Discussing the architecture of modern models (e.g., YOLO, Mask R-CNN, SAM) and their trade-offs.
3.  **Building Vision-Language Agents:** How do you design an agent that takes a visual input and performs a multi-step reasoning task using an LLM?
4.  **Edge Optimization:** Explaining your process for deploying vision models to resource-constrained devices (ONNX, Quantization, Pruning).
5.  **Agentic AI Frameworks:** Deep dive into LangChain or AutoGen for planning and tool-calling in a vision context.
6.  **Feature Selection:** How do you determine which visual features are most relevant for a specific downstream task?

## Technical "Must-Haves" for the Interview
- 3+ years of dedicated **Computer Vision** work.
- 1+ year of experience with **Agentic AI** development.
- Strong proficiency in **PyTorch** and **OpenCV**.
- Knowledge of **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)** like CLIP or LLaVA.
