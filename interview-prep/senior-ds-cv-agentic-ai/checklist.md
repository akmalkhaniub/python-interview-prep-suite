# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Senior DS (CV & Agentic AI)

## Day 1: Traditional Computer Vision (OpenCV)
- [ ] Review **Image Filters** (Canny, Sobel) and **Feature Matching** (ORB, SIFT).
- [ ] Study **Geometry & Calibration** (Homographies, Camera Matrix).
- [ ] Practice a simple tracking task using **Optical Flow** in OpenCV.

## Day 2: Deep Learning Architectures
- [ ] Review **Object Detection** evolution (YOLO vs. Faster R-CNN).
- [ ] Study **Segmentation** models (U-Net, Mask R-CNN, SAM).
- [ ] Research **Vision Transformers (ViT)** and their performance vs. CNNs.

## Day 3: Feature Engineering & Embeddings
- [ ] Review **HOG, LBP, and Gabor filters** for handcrafted feature extraction.
- [ ] Study **Contrastive Learning** (CLIP) and how to use vision embeddings.
- [ ] Practice **Dimensionality Reduction** (PCA) on a visual dataset.

## Day 4: Agentic AI & Orchestration
- [ ] Review **LangChain Agents** and the **ReAct reasoning pattern**.
- [ ] Study **AutoGen** for multi-agent collaboration.
- [ ] Design a high-level workflow for a "Vision Agent" that uses an LLM to reason over visual data.

## Day 5: VLMs & Multimodal AI
- [ ] Review **CLIP, BLIP, and LLaVA** architectures.
- [ ] Study **Multimodal RAG** and vector databases for vision.
- [ ] Research **Zero-shot classification** using VLMs.

## Day 6: Edge Deployment & MLOps
- [ ] Review **ONNX Runtime** and model conversion steps.
- [ ] Study **Quantization (Int8)** and **Pruning** for edge AI.
- [ ] Research **MLflow** for experiment tracking and model registry.

## Day 7: Pitch & Architectural Leadership
- [ ] Finalize your **CV + Agentic AI** success stories (Detection -> Reasoning -> Outcome).
- [ ] Prepare for **Architectural questions**: How do you choose the right model for a specific business problem?
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain the trade-offs between "Handcrafted Features" and "Deep Embeddings" to a non-technical stakeholder.
