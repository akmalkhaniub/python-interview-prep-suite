# Engineering Lead (WhizPay)

## About WhizPay
WhizPay is building modern global payment infrastructure powered by stablecoins and programmable finance. We enable businesses to move money seamlessly across borders, from fiat onramps to crypto-based settlement and local currency payouts.

## About the Role
We’re hiring an Engineering Lead to own the architecture, scalability, and reliability of a financial-grade platform powering cross-border payments, wallets, and ledger systems. This is a hands-on leadership role.

## Responsibilities
*   **Architecture:** Lead technical decision-making across backend (Node.js/TypeScript) and frontend systems.
*   **Ledger Systems:** Design and maintain a reliable, auditable ledger across USD, USDC, and local currencies.
*   **Integrations:** Own integrations with custodial wallets, payment aggregators, and banking rails.
*   **Reliability:** Ensure system uptime and incident response for financial-grade infrastructure.
*   **Leadership:** Lead and mentor engineers, perform code reviews, and drive hiring.

## Requirements
*   **Experience:** 5+ years backend engineering, 2+ years in a senior or lead role.
*   **Tech Stack:** Deep expertise in Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and ORMs (TypeORM).
*   **Financial Knowledge:** Strong understanding of ledgers, reconciliation, and double-entry bookkeeping.
*   **Security:** Strong fundamentals in encryption, secrets management, and audit trails.
*   **Mindset:** Clean code, strong architectural decisions, and extreme attention to detail with financial data.

## Nice to Have
*   Blockchain or stablecoin systems (USDC).
*   Experience in cross-border payments or remittance.
*   Exposure to emerging markets (Pakistan, Bangladesh).
*   Startup experience (0→1 systems).
