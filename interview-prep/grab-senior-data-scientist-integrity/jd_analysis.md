# Job Description Analysis: Senior Data Scientist (Integrity) - Grab

## Role Overview
**Company:** Grab (Southeast Asia's Superapp)
**Position:** Senior Data Scientist
**Team:** Integrity (Fraud, Risk, Digital Identity, Safety)
**Location:** Singapore (One North)
**Focus:** Computer Vision (CV), Multimodal LLMs, OCR, KYC Verification.
**Level:** 3+ years experience.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Computer Vision & Visual Intelligence
- **Focus:** Precise OCR, document/image information extraction.
- **Applications:** KYC (Know Your Customer) verification, user onboarding.
- **Modern Tech:** Multimodal LLMs (mLLMs), Vision LLMs (vLLMs), and post-training techniques.

### 2. End-to-End AI Engineering
- **Modelling Lifecycle:** Data exploration -> Model development -> Production deployment -> Model refresh.
- **Architecture:** Designing high-throughput document processing pipelines.
- **Scaling:** Building models that operate at massive scale (Grab's user base).

### 3. Production & Optimization
- **Deployment:** Deploying into high-throughput environments.
- **Monitoring:** Implementing production metrics and iterating on latency and efficiency.
- **Tools:** Python, Spark, SQL, TensorFlow, PyTorch.

### 4. Research & Innovation
- **Prototyping:** Translating cutting-edge academic research (CV/mLLM) into product impact.
- **Post-training:** Fine-tuning and optimizing state-of-the-art models.

## Key Requirements
- **Experience:** 3+ years in tech companies or academic institutions.
- **Skills:** Proficiency in Python, Spark, SQL; Deep learning (TF/PyTorch); Version control (Git).
- **Mindset:** Hands-on, research-driven, and clear communicator.

## Interview Themes to Prepare
1.  **KYC Automation:** Designing a robust system to verify identity documents across different countries with varying formats and lighting conditions.
2.  **Multimodal LLMs for OCR:** How to use a Vision LLM to extract data from blurry or complex documents compared to traditional OCR.
3.  **Scalability & Latency:** Deploying a deep learning model for real-time KYC without introducing significant user friction (latency).
4.  **Handling Fraud:** Identifying adversarial attacks on identity verification (e.g., printed photos, digital screen replays, or deepfakes).
5.  **Post-training Optimization:** Techniques for fine-tuning a pre-trained Vision LLM for specific Grab-related document types.
