# Interview Prep Checklist: Senior Data Scientist (Grab)

## Phase 1: CV & OCR Deep Dive (Days 1-2)
- [ ] **Review OCR SOTA:** Research DBNet and Attention-based recognition models.
- [ ] **Document AI Models:** Deep dive into LayoutLMv3 and Donut architectures.
- [ ] **Preprocessing:** Review image enhancement techniques (denoising, glare removal).

## Phase 2: Multimodal LLMs (Days 3-4)
- [ ] **Vision LLM Architectures:** Understand LLaVA-style projection layers and training stages.
- [ ] **Fine-tuning Techniques:** Review LoRA and QLoRA for Vision-Language models.
- [ ] **Inference Optimization:** Learn about TensorRT and model quantization for CV.

## Phase 3: Fraud & KYC Context (Days 5-6)
- [ ] **Identity Verification Trends:** Research "Liveness Detection" and "Presentation Attack Detection."
- [ ] **Regional Context:** Understand Southeast Asian document diversity (ID cards from Indo, Thai, VN, etc.).
- [ ] **System Design:** Whiteboard an end-to-end KYC pipeline including a human-in-the-loop stage.

## Phase 4: Grab Culture & Impact (Day 7)
- [ ] **Grab Core Values:** Review "Heart, Hunger, Honour, Humility" and how you've demonstrated them.
- [ ] **STAR Stories (Integrity):** Focus on stories about fraud prevention, risk mitigation, or scaling AI.
- [ ] **Technical Communication:** Practice explaining "Model Trade-offs" (e.g., Accuracy vs. Latency) to stakeholders.

---
**Key Terms to Master:**
- **KYC:** Know Your Customer.
- **MRZ:** Machine Readable Zone.
- **mLLM/vLLM:** Multimodal/Vision Large Language Models.
- **SOTA:** State of the Art.
