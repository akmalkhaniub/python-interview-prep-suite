# Senior Data Scientist (Integrity) - Grab

This module is tailored for the Senior Data Scientist position within Grab's Integrity team. The role focuses on Computer Vision and Multimodal LLMs to solve high-stakes problems in identity verification (KYC), fraud detection, and safety.

## 📁 Module Contents

1.  **[JD Analysis](./jd_analysis.md)**: Analysis of Grab's integrity mission, visual intelligence requirements, and end-to-end modelling lifecycle.
2.  **[Technical Study Guide](./study_guide.md)**: Deep dives into OCR SOTA, Vision Transformers, Multimodal LLMs (mLLMs), and anti-spoofing techniques.
3.  **[Preparation Checklist](./checklist.md)**: A 7-day plan focusing on CV research, KYC system design, and Grab's core values.

## 🛠️ Key Technologies
- **AI/ML:** Computer Vision, OCR, Multimodal LLMs (vLLMs), TensorFlow, PyTorch.
- **Tools:** Python, Spark, SQL, Git-flow.
- **Applications:** KYC, Identity Verification, Fraud & Risk mitigation.
- **Optimization:** TensorRT, Quantization, High-throughput deployment.

## 🛡️ Strategic Focus: Trust & Safety
- Guarding the superapp's users from fraud and risk.
- Scaling onboarding operations for millions of users across Southeast Asia.
- Translating cutting-edge CV research into tangible product impact.
