## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, Node.js, WebSockets, Twilio, Vapi, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Claude/GPT.
- **Concepts:** Latency Optimization (<800ms), Barge-in/Interruptions, Agentic Tool Use, Conversation Design, TCPA Compliance.
- **Mission:** Building autonomous voice agents that handle booking, support, and sales end-to-end.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on **Latency**—it's the most highlighted requirement. Know exactly how you optimize for sub-second response times.
- Be prepared to discuss **Barge-in** logic; handling interruptions is what separates a demo from a production system.
- Highlight your **Tool Use** experience; the company wants agents that *do* things, not just talk.
- Emphasize your **Production Track Record**; mention the scale and real-world impact of the agents you've shipped.
- Mention your familiarity with **LangGraph or MCP**; these "Next-gen" tools show you are at the cutting edge of agentic AI.
