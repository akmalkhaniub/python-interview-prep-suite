## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Flutter (Mobile + Web), BLoC, React Native (Expo), REST/GraphQL.
- **Experience:** 7-10 years (Senior/Lead level expectations).
- **Environment:** Remote (Permanent), AEST work hours.

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **BLoC** expertise—it is a "mandatory" requirement for the Flutter stack.
- Highlight any experience with **Flutter Web**, as it's specifically called out.
- Be prepared to discuss **scalable architecture** (Clean Architecture) given the 7-10 years experience requirement.
- Mention your comfort with **AEST working hours** (10 AM - 6 PM) early in the conversation.
