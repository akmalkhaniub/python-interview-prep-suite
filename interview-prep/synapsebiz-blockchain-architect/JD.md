# Blockchain Architect - Synapsebiz

## About the job
**Location: Islamabad, Pakistan (Hybrid / Work from home options)**

### Company Description
Synapsebiz is dedicated to building digital systems that place trust, security, and resilience at the core of technology. The company focuses on scalable, user-centric, and future-ready solutions that serve individuals, institutions, and businesses. Working at the intersection of advanced cryptography, modern software design, and responsible innovation, Synapsebiz aims to create infrastructures that endure and systems that adapt. The team emphasizes technologies that earn confidence through verifiable proof rather than assumptions. Applicants who care about secure platforms, human-centered design, and long-term digital impact will find a strong mission alignment at Synapsebiz.

### Role Description
This is a full-time Blockchain Architect role based in Islamabad with a hybrid work arrangement, combining on-site collaboration with the option for some work from home. The Blockchain Architect will design, prototype, and implement blockchain-based architectures and protocols that support secure, scalable, and resilient platforms. 

### Key Responsibilities
- Define system and data models, selecting appropriate blockchain frameworks, and integrating blockchain components with back-end services and APIs.
- Collaborate with software developers, product stakeholders, and security specialists to translate requirements into robust technical designs, as well as conducting code reviews and performance tuning.
- Evaluate emerging blockchain and cryptography technologies, establish best practices, and contribute to technical documentation and architectural standards.

### Qualifications
- **Foundational Blockchain Knowledge:** Strong foundational knowledge in Blockchain and Cryptocurrency, including consensus mechanisms, token models, smart contracts, and distributed ledger technologies.
- **Applied Cryptography:** Expertise in public-key cryptography, digital signatures (ECDSA, Ed25519), hashing, and key management in secure, real-world systems.
- **Back-End Development:** Proficiency in Back-End Web Development and Software Development, including RESTful APIs, microservices, and integration with blockchain networks.
- **Architectural Design:** Experience designing scalable, secure system architectures and translating business requirements into technical solutions.
- **Language Stack:** Proficiency in one or more modern programming languages commonly used in blockchain ecosystems (e.g., Go, Rust, Java, TypeScript/Node.js, Solidity).
- **Deployment & Cloud:** Comfort working with cloud environments, containers, and CI/CD pipelines to deploy and maintain blockchain-enabled services.
- **Communication:** Ability to write clear technical documentation, communicate complex concepts to diverse stakeholders, and collaborate in cross-functional teams.
- **Education:** Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- **Security & Performance:** Familiarity with security best practices, threat modeling, and performance optimization for distributed systems is highly beneficial.
