# AI Engineer at HALA (Fintech)

## About the job
**Location:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia / Dubai, United Arab Emirates (Remote, Hybrid, and In-Office options supported)
**Company:** HALA (Licensed fintech company empowering SMEs with digital payment and expense management platforms)

### Role Overview
HALA is seeking a skilled AI Engineer to build, evaluate, and integrate AI/ML models and generative workflows into our Core Fintech products. You will be responsible for creating AI-powered financial chatbots, merchant copilots, document extraction pipelines, and automated SME transaction analysis tools.

This role requires a combination of strong machine learning foundations (PyTorch/TensorFlow, scikit-learn) and practical experience with LLMs, RAG, NLP embeddings, and secure vector search.

---

### Core Responsibilities
- **AI Solution Design:** Build and deploy machine learning, NLP, and generative AI models (such as financial assistants, text extractors, and automated categorizations).
- **API Integration:** Integrate models via REST/gRPC APIs into HALA's transaction systems and microservices.
- **Model Fine-Tuning:** Fine-tune, evaluate, and monitor pre-trained LLM weights (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, or Vertex AI).
- **RAG Infrastructure:** Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines over merchant transaction data, company policies, and financial documents.
- **Data Engineering:** Process, clean, and analyze complex transaction tables to prepare datasets for model training and classification.
- **Compliance & Security:** Ensure AI models adhere to strict financial data regulations, secure handling of Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and data governance standards.

---

### Must-Have Qualifications
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related quantitative field.
- **Experience:** 2–5 years of professional experience in software engineering, machine learning, or data science.
- **Programming Language:** Expert in Python.
- **AI/ML Frameworks:** Practical experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or Scikit-learn.
- **Generative AI Stack:** Deep knowledge of Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, embeddings, and vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Qdrant, PGVector).
- **Fintech Compliance:** Solid understanding of financial regulations, data isolation, and security best practices (GDPR, local MENAP financial compliance).
