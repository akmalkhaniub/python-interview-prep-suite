# Interview Prep Checklist: Full Stack Marketing Engineer

## Phase 1: Knowledge Consolidation (Days 1-2)
- [ ] **Review Meta CAPI Docs:** Understand the `event_id` and `user_data` hashing requirements.
- [ ] **Research CRM APIs:** Spend 30 mins each on GoHighLevel (GHL) and Close CRM developer docs.
- [ ] **AI Marketing Trends:** Review current use cases for LLMs in ad copy and lead scoring.

## Phase 2: Technical Portfolio/Hands-on (Days 3-4)
- [ ] **Next.js Dashboard:** Sketch out a design for a "Master Marketing Dashboard" that tracks ROAS and CPL.
- [ ] **Python Automation Script:** Write a small script that takes a CSV of leads and uses OpenAI to categorize them.
- [ ] **Webhook Handler:** Build a simple FastAPI endpoint that accepts a JSON payload and logs it (simulating a CRM webhook).

## Phase 3: Interview Strategy (Days 5-6)
- [ ] **STAR Stories:** Prepare 3 stories about building internal tools that improved efficiency.
- [ ] **Technical Deep Dive:** Be ready to explain the flow of data from a Lead Form -> Python Backend -> CRM -> Meta CAPI.
- [ ] **Business Context:** Understand the "Builder" vs "Strategist" distinction mentioned in the JD.

## Phase 4: Final Review (Day 7)
- [ ] **Mock Interview:** Practice explaining "Deduplication" and "Attribution" in simple terms.
- [ ] **Review JD Requirements:** Ensure you can speak to all "Non-Negotiable" items (React, Python, CAPI, CRM).

---
**Key KPIs to Memorize:**
- **ROAS:** Return on Ad Spend (Revenue / Ad Spend)
- **CPL:** Cost Per Lead (Ad Spend / Total Leads)
- **CAC:** Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV:** Lifetime Value
