# Job Description Analysis: Full Stack Engineer (Marketing Agency)

## Role Overview
**Company:** Fast-growing Marketing Agency (150+ Home Service Businesses)
**Position:** Full Stack Engineer
**Location:** Remote (US Hours - EST)
**Focus:** AI, Automation, Internal Tools, Ad Tech, CRM Integration.
**Level:** 4–6+ years (Hands-on Builder).

## Core Competencies

### 1. Internal Tool Development (Next.js/Python)
- **Frontend:** Building marketing dashboards and reporting systems using **Next.js** and **React**.
- **Backend:** Scalable solutions using **Python**.
- **UX/UI:** Designing tools that media buyers and operators can use effectively to reduce manual work.

### 2. Marketing Tech & Ad Platforms
- **Tracking:** Expert knowledge of **Meta Pixel** and **Conversion API (CAPI)**.
- **Data Integrity:** Ensuring event tracking, deduplication, and attribution accuracy.
- **Reporting:** Building pipelines from Ad Platforms (Meta, Google) -> Backend -> Dashboards (ROAS, CPL focus).

### 3. AI & Automation
- **Generative AI:** Integrating **OpenAI APIs** for ad copy and creative generation.
- **Predictive AI:** Lead scoring and qualification models.
- **Workflow Automation:** Replacing manual processes with AI-driven insights and automated reporting.

### 4. CRM & Lead Lifecycle
- **Platforms:** **GoHighLevel (GHL)**, **Close CRM**, **HubSpot**.
- **Integration:** Connecting lead capture forms to CRMs and then to conversion tracking (CAPI/Offline Events).
- **Automation:** Lead routing, pipeline management, and conversion syncing.

### 5. Execution Mindset
- **Speed:** Rapid iteration and shipping production-ready code.
- **Problem Solving:** Solving bottlenecks for media buyers and campaign operators.

## Key Requirements
- **Experience:** 4–6+ years of software engineering.
- **Stack:** React/Next.js + Python + APIs/Webhooks.
- **Domain:** **Marketing / Ad Tech** is non-negotiable.
- **Tools:** Experience with Meta Pixel, CAPI, and CRM automation.

## Interview Themes to Prepare
1.  **System Design:** Designing a lead attribution pipeline that handles Meta CAPI and CRM offline events.
2.  **API Integration:** Building a robust webhook handler in Python for GoHighLevel to sync lead status to a custom dashboard.
3.  **AI Implementation:** How to build an automated ad copy generator using LangChain/OpenAI that respects brand voice.
4.  **Data Deduplication:** Strategies for ensuring Meta Pixel and CAPI events don't double-count conversions.
5.  **Performance Optimization:** Building a Next.js dashboard that aggregates data from multiple ad APIs without hitting rate limits.
