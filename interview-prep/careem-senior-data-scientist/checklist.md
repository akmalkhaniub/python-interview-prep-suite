# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Senior Data Scientist (Careem)

## Day 1: Marketplace Science & Matching
- [ ] Review the **Hungarian Algorithm** and **Batch Matching** strategies.
- [ ] Study **Surge Pricing** and **Supply-Demand Elasticity**.
- [ ] Research **H3 Hexagonal Indexing** for geospatial analysis.

## Day 2: Big Data Mastery (Spark/SQL)
- [ ] Review **Spark SQL** optimization techniques (Partitioning, JOIN strategies).
- [ ] Practice complex **SQL / Hive** queries for feature engineering on large datasets.
- [ ] Study **BigQuery** best practices for exploratory data analysis (EDA).

## Day 3: Ranking & Recommendations
- [ ] Review **Learning to Rank (LTR)** approaches (Pointwise, Pairwise, Listwise).
- [ ] Study **XGBoost Ranker** and **NDCG** metrics.
- [ ] Practice designing a recommendation system for the "Everything App" services.

## Day 4: Experimentation (AB Testing)
- [ ] Review **Sample size calculation**, **Power**, and **P-values**.
- [ ] Study **Switchback Testing** and **Cluster-based randomization** for marketplaces.
- [ ] Research how to handle **Network Effects** in ride-hailing experiments.

## Day 5: Causal Inference & Uplift Modeling
- [ ] Review **Uplift Modeling** techniques (Two-model approach, Meta-learners).
- [ ] Study **Propensity Score Matching** and its use in causal analysis.
- [ ] Practice explaining the "Four Quadrants" of user behavior in uplift modeling.

## Day 6: Advanced ML & Reinforcement Learning
- [ ] Review **Multi-armed Bandits (MAB)** for real-time optimization.
- [ ] Study **Time Series forecasting** (LSTMs, Prophet) for demand prediction.
- [ ] Research **Reinforcement Learning** applications in dynamic pricing.

## Day 7: Product Leadership & Pitch
- [ ] Finalize your **Marketplace success stories**: Matching, pricing, or experimentation.
- [ ] Prepare your "AI Vision" for Careem: How can AI transform customer acquisition?
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain the **Root Cause** of a metric shift to a non-technical Product Manager.
