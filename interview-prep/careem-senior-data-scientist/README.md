## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, SQL, Spark, Hive, BigQuery, Hadoop.
- **Concepts:** Matching, Surge Pricing, Uplift Modeling, Switchback Testing, Learning to Rank.
- **Domain:** Marketplace (Ride-hailing, Delivery, Payments).

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **Marketplace Economics**—Careem is essentially a matching engine.
- Be prepared for **Big Data technicals**; they emphasize Spark and Hive for production-grade algorithms.
- Highlight your **Experimental Design** skills; marketplace experiments are much harder than standard web AB tests.
- Prepare to discuss **Uplift Modeling**; it's a key requirement for their customer engagement strategy.
- Emphasize your **Product Leadership**; you're expected to drive recommendations that influence business decisions.
