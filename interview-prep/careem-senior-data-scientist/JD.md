About the job
About the Company
Careem is building the Everything App for the greater Middle East — making it easy to move around, order food and groceries, manage payments, and more. Our purpose is simple: to simplify and improve people’s lives and build an awesome organisation that inspires.

About The Team
The Careem Data Science team’s mission is to drive competitive value from data at scale through building AI models to optimize user experiences, decision making, operational efficiencies, and lead the region’s AI ecosystem. You will be working with and leading the top data science talent of the region, leveraging AI to solve the region’s day-to-day problems, through state-of-the-art machine learning, optimization, deep learning, and RL models.

What you'll do:
Build a long-term vision on how we can rethink our customer acquisition and engagement strategies.
Drive exploratory analysis to understand the ecosystem, user behavior, and identify new levers to move metrics.
Shape and influence data/ML models to optimize the product experience.
Provide product leadership by sharing data-based recommendations.
Implement scalable machine learning algorithms that will be used in production on big data.
Design and run randomized controlled experiments (AB tests), analyze resulting data, and communicate results.

What you'll need:
Experience in data mining, predictive modeling, time series analysis, machine learning, Big Data methodologies, transformation and cleaning of both structured and unstructured data.
Advanced degree in a quantitative discipline (Physics, Statistics, Math, CS).
Solid knowledge of marketplace problems in ride hailing and delivery services.
Solid knowledge of AB testing, classic ML and DL.
Solid understanding of recommendations, learning to rank, uplift modeling and matching problems.
Proficiency and demonstrated experience in: Python, SQL, Spark, Hive.
Experience with database technologies (Hadoop, BigQuery, Amazon EMR, Hive, Oracle).
Experience with BI and visualization tools (Tableau) along with geospatial data processing skills is a plus.
