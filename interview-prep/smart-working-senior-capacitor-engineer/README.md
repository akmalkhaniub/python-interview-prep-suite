## Role Focus
- **Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, Capacitor.
- **Key Task:** Leading the migration from Angular/Ionic to a modern React architecture.
- **Responsibility:** Owning the end-to-end release process (App Store/Play Store).

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **autonomy** and experience in making architectural decisions.
- Be prepared to discuss **real-world scale** challenges and how you've solved them.
- Highlight any experience with **Stripe**, **Firebase**, and **CI/CD** pipelines.
