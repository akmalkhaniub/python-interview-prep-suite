## Role Focus
- **Stack:** React Native (Expo), Redux, C++, Node.js, Swift/Kotlin.
- **Domain:** Crypto Wallets, DeFi, Stablecoins (USDT).
- **Leadership:** Leading small teams, driving architecture, and mentoring engineers.

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **low-level native experience** (C++/Swift/Kotlin)—this is a major differentiator for Tether.
- Be prepared to discuss **security-first development**, especially around private key management and transaction signing.
- Highlight your **full-stack versatility**—mentioning that you can modify a Node.js backend to support mobile features is a big plus.
- Prepare to discuss **high-performance patterns**—Tether's apps need to be light-weight and intuitive.
