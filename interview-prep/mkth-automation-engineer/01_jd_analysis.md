# JD Analysis: Automation Engineer (MKTH Solutions)
## Company: MKTH Solutions | Location: Clifton, Karachi | Focus: Workflow Optimization

### 1. The Core Requirement: Workflow Mastery
MKTH is looking for someone who can take a business process and digitize/automate it. This is less about "AI research" and more about **Systems Integration**.
- **Low-Code/No-Code:** Make.com, Zapier, N8n.
- **High-Code/APIs:** Direct API integrations, JSON parsing, Webhooks.
- **Optimization:** Making workflows faster, cheaper, and more reliable.

- **Client Visibility:** The ability to explain a technical workflow to a client is a major plus.
