# Senior Artificial Intelligence Engineer at Hubaix (Texas)

## About the job
**Remote | Contractual | Part Time**

### Company: Hubaix (Texas)

**About Hubaix:**
Hubaix is a Texas-based AI and software development company building intelligent platforms that drive automation, personalization, and business transformation. We specialize in creating AI solutions, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, and cutting-edge voice and text-based agents for businesses across health & wellness, fintech, and enterprise sectors.

### Role Overview:
We are hiring a Senior Artificial Intelligence Engineer for a Remote Contractual role. You will focus on building AI-driven voice bots, integrating advanced speech technologies, and embedding LLMs into scalable AI products.

Hands-on experience with Voice Bots, STT (Speech-to-Text), TTS (Text-to-Speech), and LLMs is mandatory.
Experience with Real-Time Voice Bots and Multi-tenant SaaS platforms is a strong plus.
This role is ideal for technically strong engineers who are passionate about voice AI, LLM-based systems, and building real-world scalable solutions.

### Key Responsibilities:
- Design, develop, and deploy AI models powering voice bots and conversational agents.
- Integrate Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) technologies for voice interactions.
- Implement and fine-tune Large Language Models (LLMs) for enhanced intelligence.
- Architect and optimize AI systems within multi-tenant SaaS environments.
- Ensure AI models meet standards of scalability, security, and low-latency performance.
- Write clean, modular, and well-documented code.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on system architecture, deployments, and improvements.
- Stay updated with the latest research and best practices in AI, LLMs, and voice technologies.

### Must-Have Qualifications: 
- 5+ years of professional AI/ML engineering / Software Development experience.
- 3+ years of professional LLM experience.
- Proven experience with:
  - Voice Bot development.
  - Speech-to-Text (STT) systems.
  - Text-to-Speech (TTS) systems.
  - Large Language Model (LLM) integration and fine-tuning.
- Strong programming skills in Python and experience with AI/ML frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, or LangChain LanGraph.
- Experience deploying AI models using AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Solid understanding of building scalable, production-grade AI systems.
- Strong problem-solving, debugging, and communication skills.

### Bonus Skills (Preferred):
- Experience working on Real-Time Voice Bots (low-latency conversational agents).
- Experience building or working with Multi-tenant SaaS platforms.
- Experience hosting LLMs or speech models offline.
- Familiarity with HIPAA, GDPR, or compliance-driven application development.
- Exposure to agentic AI systems and intelligent automation solutions.

### Perks:
- Competitive compensation.
- Fully remote working environment.
- Opportunity to build and scale cutting-edge AI and voice tech solutions.
- High ownership and growth opportunities within projects.
