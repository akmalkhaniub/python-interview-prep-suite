# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Data Scientist (LineSlip)

## Day 1: LLM Fine-Tuning & PEFT
- [ ] Review the math and implementation of **LoRA and QLoRA**.
- [ ] Experiment with **Unsloth** for fine-tuning a small model (e.g., Llama-3-8B).
- [ ] Study **PEFT** library by Hugging Face for parameter-efficient updates.

## Day 2: RAG & Vector Search
- [ ] Review **Chunking strategies** for long documents (Insurance Binders).
- [ ] Practice building a RAG pipeline with **FAISS** and **SentenceTransformers**.
- [ ] Study **Hybrid Search** and **Reranking** techniques.

## Day 3: Agentic AI & Orchestration
- [ ] Master **LangChain (LCEL)** for building extraction chains.
- [ ] Review **LangSmith** for tracing and evaluating agents.
- [ ] Practice a **Self-Reflective Agent** pattern for data validation.

## Day 4: Azure Cloud & MLOps
- [ ] Review **Azure Machine Learning (AML)** job submission and model registration.
- [ ] Study **AKS (Azure Kubernetes Service)** deployment patterns for LLMs.
- [ ] Review **MLflow** for experiment tracking and metric logging.

## Day 5: Context & Infrastructure
- [ ] Review strategies for **Large Context Windows** (Flash Attention 2, Sliding Windows).
- [ ] Practice **Dockerizing** a Python-based ML training script.
- [ ] Study **DeepSpeed** for distributed training on multi-GPU setups.

## Day 6: Linux & Shell Scripting
- [ ] Practice common **Linux Shell commands** for data manipulation (sed, awk, grep).
- [ ] Write a script to automate **Model Retraining** triggers.
- [ ] Review **Docker Compose** for local testing of multi-service RAG systems.

## Day 7: Pitch & Soft Skills
- [ ] Prepare your "4+ Years Experience" and "2+ Years Stability" stories.
- [ ] Finalize your pitch on why you are the perfect fit for **Insurance Mining**.
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain **Unsloth optimization** to a non-technical stakeholder.
