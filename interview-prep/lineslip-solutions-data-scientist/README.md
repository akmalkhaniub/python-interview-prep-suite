## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, Azure, Docker, AKS, LangChain, LangSmith.
- **Concepts:** LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT, Unsloth, RAG, Vector Search, Large Context Windows.
- **Mission:** Extracting data from binders, policies, and insurance proposals.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **Unsloth and LoRA** aspects—this is a key differentiator for this role.
- Be prepared for a **whiteboard session**; they explicitly mention this after an automated test.
- Highlight your **stability** (2+ years at one employer); the JD stresses this as a filter.
- Emphasize your **Agentic AI** knowledge (LangChain/LangSmith); it's a highly preferred skill.
- Mention your experience with **Insurance-specific data** if you have any; it's the core of their business.
