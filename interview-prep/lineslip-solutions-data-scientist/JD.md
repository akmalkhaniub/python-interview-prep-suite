About the job
LineSlip Solutions: Data Scientist (Insurance Mining)

We are seeking a highly skilled Data Scientist to join our innovative SaaS-based insurance mining platform. We have experienced record growth and are excited to grow the team. The ideal candidate will have industrial real world production expertise in fine-tuning large language models (LLMs), deep learning, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

Key Requirements:
Candidate must have at least 4 years of experience with title ML Engineer or Data Scientist.
Candidate must have 2 years continuous employment with the same employer.
Candidates must have completed a Stem related degree.
Recent grads are NOT a fit for this role.

Key Responsibilities:
1. Model Development & Fine-tuning:
Fine-tune and deploy large-scale LLMs (GPT, Llama, Falcon, Qwen) to extract insights from structured and unstructured insurance data.
Experience with agentic AI (Langchain/Langsmith preference).
Leverage LoRA, PEFT, and QLoRA to optimize performance.
Implement large-context-window models for long insurance documents.

2. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Systems:
Develop RAG pipelines integrating with external knowledge sources (Pinecone, FAISS, Azure Cognitive Search).
Optimize embedding-based document search for large insurance databases.

3. Containerization & Cloud Deployment:
Design and manage containerized environments using Docker and Docker Compose.
Deploy and orchestrate LLMs in Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) and Azure Machine Learning (AML).
Implement distributed training using Azure Databricks, DeepSpeed, or Ray.

4. Model Performance & Monitoring:
Monitor and improve performance (F1-score, perplexity, inference time) using tools like Unsloth.
Implement MLOps best practices (MLflow, Azure Pipelines).

Technical Skills:
Expert in Python.
Familiarity with Unsloth for large LLM fine-tuning.
Strong experience in embedding models and distributed training.
Strong Linux experience and shell scripting.

Preferred Qualifications:
Experience working with insurance workflows (underwriting, claims analysis).
Familiarity with long-document models (BigBird, Memorizing Transformers, Llama2-Long).
Knowledge of Azure OpenAI Services.
