## Role Focus
- **Stack:** TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, React, Postgres, AWS (Lambda, SQS, S3).
- **Concepts:** Amazon SP-API, Data Pipelines, Table Partitioning, Event-Driven Architecture, AI Agents.
- **Mission:** Transforming raw Amazon report data into automated, multi-tenant actionable insights.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **Data Engineering track record**—the JD explicitly asks for "real examples" of pipelines you've built.
- Be prepared for a **deep dive into Postgres**; they emphasize partitioning and indexing for large datasets.
- Highlight your **Product Awareness**; GNO Partners values engineers who understand why they are building the pipelines.
- Mention your familiarity with **Amazon APIs** if you have it; it's a "Nice to Have" that will set you apart.
- Emphasize your ability to **Work Across the Stack**; you'll be building report tools end-to-end.
