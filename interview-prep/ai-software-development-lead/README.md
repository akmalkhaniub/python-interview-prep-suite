## Role Focus
- **Leadership:** Leading/mentoring developers and architects (10+ years exp).
- **Innovation:** Championing AI adoption across the entire SDLC and product roadmap.
- **Architecture:** Designing AI-integrated microservices and scalable cloud solutions.
- **Autonomy:** High-level ownership of end-to-end product development.

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **leadership journey**: How you've evolved from a senior dev to an AI-first lead.
- Be prepared to discuss the **ROI of AI**: How AI initiatives drive real business value.
- Highlight your experience in **managing ambiguity** and translating vision into technical delivery.
