# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: AI / Software Development Lead

## Day 1: Leadership & Mentorship
- [ ] Review leadership frameworks (e.g., Situational Leadership).
- [ ] Prepare 3 "Mentorship" stories: One success, one failure, one long-term growth story.
- [ ] Practice explaining your strategy for leading a high-performing engineering team.

## Day 2: AI-Assisted Development
- [ ] Research the current state of AI-assisted coding tools (Copilot, etc.).
- [ ] Draft a plan for "AI Transformation": How to introduce AI tools into a legacy SDLC.
- [ ] Study techniques for measuring engineering productivity gains from AI.

## Day 3: LLM Orchestration & RAG
- [ ] Review LangChain or Semantic Kernel core concepts.
- [ ] Practice designing a RAG system for internal documentation.
- [ ] Study OpenAI's "Function Calling" and how it enables agentic behavior.

## Day 4: Cloud-Native AI & Scaling
- [ ] Review Kubernetes and Docker basics for deploying AI services.
- [ ] Study AWS Bedrock or Azure OpenAI service architectures.
- [ ] Research strategies for handling high-latency AI API calls (Streaming, Caching).

## Day 5: System Design (Traditional + AI)
- [ ] Practice a "System Design" interview: "Design a scalable AI-powered customer support platform."
- [ ] Study observability for AI (Logging prompts, tracking costs, monitoring hallucinations).
- [ ] Review fallback patterns for non-deterministic AI outputs.

## Day 6: Business Stakeholders & ROI
- [ ] Practice translating AI jargon into business value for non-technical leaders.
- [ ] Research how to calculate the ROI of AI-driven product features.
- [ ] Prepare "Conflict Resolution" stories involving technical vs. business priorities.

## Day 7: Mock Interview & Roadmap
- [ ] Conduct a mock "Lead Interview": Roadmap planning for a new AI-focused product.
- [ ] Final review of AI trends (Agents, Multi-modal, Small Language Models).
- [ ] Practice "Ambiguity Handling": How you deal with vague product visions.
