## Role Focus
- **Philosophy:** Syntax-free. Architecture-first. AI-driven 100% of the time.
- **Tools:** Claude Max, Cursor, Remote Dev Boxes.
- **Concepts:** Self-improving Agent Loops, Autonomous BI, Healthcare Inflation Analysis.
- **Domain:** Global Healthcare Insurance (Singapore, Thailand, France).

## Quick Tips
- Focus on **Architecture, not Code**—Novo AI explicitly values engineers who orchestrate rather than type.
- Be prepared for a **Systems-Thinking Assessment**; they are looking for "elite" minds who understand the high-leverage flow of healthcare money.
- Highlight your **Agentic AI Expertise**; mention experience with self-correction and multi-step reasoning loops.
- Emphasize your **Output-driven Mindset**; Novo AI rewards results and high-throttle AI direction.
- Mention your familiarity with **Claude and Cursor**; they are the core "arsenal" of the company.
