# Deep Dive: Engineering Leadership & Mobile System Design

Principal roles focus as much on people and process as they do on code.

## 1. Technical Leadership at Scale
*   **Mentorship:** Moving from "telling people what to do" to "teaching them how to think."
*   **Estimation:** Using PERT or Three-Point Estimation for complex mobile projects involving native modules and third-party APIs.
*   **Technical Debt:** Strategically deciding when to "ship fast" and when to "refactor for scale."

## 2. Mobile System Design Patterns
*   **Atomic Design:** Organizing components from Atoms to Pages to ensure UI consistency.
*   **Feature-Sliced Design (FSD):** A modern architectural methodology for organizing large frontend/mobile projects.
*   **Dependency Injection:** Using DI patterns in React Native (InversifyJS or simple factory patterns) to make code testable.

## 3. The GraphQL + Mobile Advantage
*   **Schema First Development:** Collaborating with backend teams to define types before implementation.
*   **Fragment Colocation:** Keeping data requirements next to the UI components that use them.
*   **Persisted Queries:** Reducing network payload size and improving security.

## 4. Hiring and Evaluation
*   **Technical Interviews:** How to assess a candidate's "depth" beyond just knowing React hooks.
*   **Cultural Fit:** Identifying candidates who align with the "flexible attitude and versatile personality" mentioned in the JD.
*   **Pair Programming:** Using live coding to evaluate problem-solving and communication skills.

## 5. Client Communication
*   **Managing Expectations:** Explaining the technical constraints of cross-platform development (e.g., why a specific iOS feature might take longer to implement in RN).
*   **Status Reporting:** Moving beyond "we are working on it" to data-driven updates (Burndown charts, Velocity metrics).

## Interview Questions
1.  "How do you handle a situation where a developer consistently misses deadlines or produces low-quality code?"
2.  "Describe your process for architectural decision-making. How do you decide between two competing technologies (e.g., GraphQL vs. REST)?"
3.  "How do you mentor a mid-level engineer who wants to transition into a lead role?"
4.  "What is your approach to 'hiring'? What specific signals do you look for in a Principal candidate?"
