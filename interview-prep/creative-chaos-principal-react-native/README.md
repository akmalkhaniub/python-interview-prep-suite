## Role Focus
- **Stack:** React Native, TypeScript, GraphQL, Swift/Objective-C, Kotlin/Java.
- **Leadership:** Leading teams, mentoring engineers, and evaluating candidates.
- **Innovation:** Bridging high-level JS logic with underlying native performance and security.

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **native background** (iOS/Android)—this JD explicitly mentions it.
- Be prepared for **high-level system design** questions; as a Principal, you are the technical authority.
- Highlight your **leadership philosophy** and how you foster a culture of quality and performance.
