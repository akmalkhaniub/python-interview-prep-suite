About the job
Applied AI Scientist
Employment Type: Full-Time
Location: Remote, Pakistan
Level: Mid to Senior (PhD Required)

About The Role
We're looking for a quantitatively-strong data professional who can bridge rigorous statistical thinking with hands-on AI development. You'll work across Pixalate's full product suite — trust & safety, ad fraud detection, brand safety, and more — using your background in statistics and ML to build intelligent agents and automated pipelines that keep our data accurate, our models honest, and our products reliable.

Responsibilities:
Analyze traffic, behavioral, and signals data to identify patterns, anomalies, and data quality issues.
Translate complex quantitative findings into clear, actionable recommendations.
Design and maintain AI agents, dashboards and reporting pipelines to track data quality.
Partner with engineering and product teams to ensure data quality and integrity.
Collaborate with AI/ML teams to close the feedback loop between QA findings and model improvements.
Support data needs across supply-side intelligence, MFA detection, seller/app ratings, and compliance.

What We're Looking For:
Must-Have:
PhD in Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related quantitative field.
3–6 years of experience in a data science, ML, or data analytics role.
Strong statistical foundation — you understand distributions, hypothesis testing, model evaluation, and where things go wrong.
Hands-on experience building with LLMs and AI agents (LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen, or similar).
Proficiency in Python and SQL.
Ability to think critically about data quality and model outputs — naturally skeptical and rigorous.

Nice-to-Have:
Prior experience in cybersecurity, fraud detection, trust & safety, or digital advertising ecosystems.
Exposure to multi-agent orchestration patterns.

About Pixalate:
Pixalate is an online trust and safety platform that protects businesses, consumers and children from deceptive, fraudulent and non-compliant mobile, CTV apps and websites. We're seeking a PhD-level AI Engineer to lead cutting-edge research in agentic AI systems, multimodal analysis, and advanced reasoning architectures.
