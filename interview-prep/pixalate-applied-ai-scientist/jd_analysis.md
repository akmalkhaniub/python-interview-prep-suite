# Job Description Analysis: Applied AI Scientist - Pixalate

## Role Overview
**Company:** Pixalate (Trust & Safety Platform)
**Position:** Applied AI Scientist
**Level:** Mid to Senior (**PhD Required**)
**Core Mission:** Bridging rigorous statistical thinking with hands-on AI development to protect businesses and consumers from ad fraud and non-compliant apps.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Rigorous Statistical Foundation
- **Expertise:** Distributions, hypothesis testing, and model evaluation.
- **Skepticism:** Ability to identify where models and data "go wrong."
- **Anomaly Detection:** Analyzing traffic and behavioral signals to find fraudulent patterns.

### 2. Agentic AI & LLM Development
- **Orchestration:** Designing AI agents and workflows using LangChain, LlamaIndex, or AutoGen.
- **Reasoning:** Building advanced reasoning architectures for automated quality assurance.
- **Tool Use:** Creating agents that can use external tools to check, flag, and audit data at scale.

### 3. Domain-Specific Intelligence
- **Ad Fraud:** Detecting multi-million dollar scams in CTV and mobile ecosystems.
- **Brand Safety:** Ensuring ads don't appear next to non-compliant or deceptive content.
- **MFA Detection:** Identifying "Made For Advertising" sites and apps.

### 4. Technical & Research Leadership
- **Collaboration:** Partnering with engineering and product to close the feedback loop between QA and ML models.
- **Communication:** Translating complex quantitative findings into actionable recommendations.
- **Multimodal:** Leading research in multimodal analysis (Text, Image, Video for compliance).

## Key Interview Themes to Prepare

1.  **The Statistical "Why":** Beyond just using ML models, can you explain the statistical distribution of fraud signals and why a specific test is appropriate?
2.  **Agentic QA at Scale:** How would you design a multi-agent system to audit millions of app listings for privacy compliance?
3.  **Detecting Subtle Anomalies:** Discussing your approach to finding low-volume, high-value fraud patterns in noisy traffic data.
4.  **Closing the Feedback Loop:** How do you turn manual "QA findings" into features or constraints for production ML models?
5.  **Multimodal Compliance:** Thinking about how to use vision and text models together to detect "spyware" behavior in children's apps.
6.  **Critical Thinking:** Discussing a time your skepticism led to the discovery of a major data quality issue or model hallucination.

## Technical "Must-Haves" for the Interview
- **PhD** in a quantitative field (Statistics, CS, Math).
- Strong proficiency in **Python** and **SQL**.
- Hands-on experience with **Agentic Frameworks** (CrewAI, AutoGen, etc.).
- Deep understanding of **Statistical Inference** and **Hypothesis Testing**.
