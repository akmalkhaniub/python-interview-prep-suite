# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: Applied AI Scientist (Pixalate)

## Day 1: Statistical Foundations & Distributions
- [ ] Review **Heavy-tailed distributions** (Pareto, Log-normal) and their role in traffic analysis.
- [ ] Practice **Hypothesis Testing** and Power analysis for non-Gaussian data.
- [ ] Study **Model Calibration** and evaluation metrics (Precision-Recall, partial AUC).

## Day 2: Anomaly Detection & Fraud Patterns
- [ ] Review **Unsupervised ML** for anomaly detection (Isolation Forests, One-class SVM).
- [ ] Research common **Ad Fraud vectors** (Spoofing, SDK Injection, MFA).
- [ ] Study the **CTV (Connected TV) ecosystem** and its specific fraud risks.

## Day 3: Agentic AI & Orchestration
- [ ] Review **LangChain, AutoGen, or CrewAI** frameworks for agent orchestration.
- [ ] Practice building a simple **Multi-agent hierarchy** for a reasoning task.
- [ ] Study the **ReAct pattern** and **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)** prompting.

## Day 4: Advanced Reasoning & Tool Use
- [ ] Review **LlamaIndex** for connecting agents to structured/unstructured data.
- [ ] Practice building a **SQL-enabled AI Agent** that can query a database to verify a hypothesis.
- [ ] Study **Memory management** for long-running agent workflows.

## Day 5: Trust, Safety & Compliance
- [ ] Review **COPPA** and **GDPR** basics related to children's apps and data privacy.
- [ ] Research **Pixalate's public research cases** (Gizmodo, Washington Post articles).
- [ ] Study **Multimodal AI** (CLIP, LLaVA) for analyzing app store assets.

## Day 6: Data Integrity & Quality Monitoring
- [ ] Review **Data Drift** and **Prior Probability Shift** detection methods.
- [ ] Practice designing a **Monitoring Pipeline** for a fraud-detection model.
- [ ] Prepare your "Skepticism stories": Times you identified major data or model failures.

## Day 7: Pitch & Research Leadership
- [ ] Finalize your **PhD Research to Production** stories.
- [ ] Prepare for **Research Leadership** questions: How do you lead cutting-edge research in a fast-moving commercial environment?
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain the **Statistical Root Cause** of a fraudulent traffic spike to a Product Manager.
