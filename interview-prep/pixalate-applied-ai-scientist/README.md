## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, SQL, LangChain, AutoGen, CrewAI, Statistics.
- **Concepts:** Anomaly Detection, Hypothesis Testing, Multi-agent Orchestration, COPPA Compliance.
- **Seniority:** PhD required, Mid to Senior level (3-6 years exp).

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **statistical rigor**—this is a "natural skepticism" role where you must explain the *why* behind the findings.
- Be prepared for **Agentic AI technicals**; they explicitly mention multi-agent orchestration and advanced reasoning.
- Highlight any experience in **Cybersecurity, Ad Fraud, or Trust & Safety**; it's a major plus.
- Mention your comfort with **fast-moving environments**; Pixalate works at the intersection of law, engineering, and research.
