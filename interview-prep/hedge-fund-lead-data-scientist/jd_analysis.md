# Job Description Analysis: Lead Data Scientist - Global Hedge Fund

## Role Overview
**Company:** Global Hedge Fund (Market Intelligence Team)
**Position:** Lead Data Scientist
**Location:** Singapore / Hong Kong focus.
**Focus:** Alpha generation, Long/Short Equities, AI-driven equity research, Agentic workflows.
**Level:** 8-12 years experience.
**Language:** Fluency in **Mandarin + English** (Critical).

## Core Competencies

### 1. Quantitative & Financial AI
- **Alpha Focus:** Building tools that directly influence P&L and investment decisions.
- **Equity Research:** Augmenting idea generation for long/short equities.
- **Signals:** Translating market inefficiencies into scalable trading signals.

### 2. Advanced AI & Agentic Workflows
- **Automation:** Designing agentic workflows to discover insights from structured and unstructured data.
- **Unstructured Data:** Processing earnings calls, filings (10-Ks, 10-Qs), news, and alternative data.
- **Stack:** Python, LLMs, ML frameworks, and data pipelines.

### 3. Execution & Commercial Mindset
- **Speed:** Rapid prototyping and iteration in a high-feedback environment.
- **Partnership:** Working side-by-side with Portfolio Managers (PMs).
- **Ownership:** High visibility, zero bureaucracy, builder mindset.

## Key Requirements
- **Experience:** 8-12 years in building AI/ML solutions for long/short equities.
- **Languages:** English and Mandarin (Mandatory for stakeholder interaction and regional data).
- **Domain:** Deep understanding of market dynamics and alpha generation.

## Interview Themes to Prepare
1.  **Agentic Insight Discovery:** Designing an agent system to automatically synthesize sentiment and key risks from hundreds of Chinese and English earnings calls.
2.  **Alpha vs. Accuracy:** Discussing a time you optimized a model for P&L impact rather than standard ML metrics (Precision/Recall).
3.  **Alternative Data Pipelines:** Building a real-time pipeline to process non-traditional data (e.g., satellite imagery, credit card data, or web traffic) into signals.
4.  **Stakeholder Management (PMs):** How to translate a PM's "market intuition" into a verifiable and scalable AI-driven tool.
5.  **Long/Short Strategy Support:** Developing tools that specifically help identify "short" candidates through anomaly detection in financial filings.
