# Interview Prep Checklist: Lead Data Scientist (Hedge Fund)

## Phase 1: Finance & Domain Mastery (Days 1-2)
- [ ] **Long/Short Equities Basics:** Review fundamental vs. quantitative research processes.
- [ ] **Alpha Generation:** Understand the concept of "Information Edge" and how AI provides it.
- [ ] **Mandarin Terminology:** Brush up on financial terms in Mandarin (e.g., balance sheet items, regulatory bodies in China).

## Phase 2: Agentic & LLM Engineering (Days 3-4)
- [ ] **Multi-Agent Design:** Practice designing workflows that use multiple LLMs for research critique.
- [ ] **Document Intelligence:** Test parsing complex financial tables from PDFs using LLMs.
- [ ] **Alternative Data:** Think of 3 novel "alt data" sources that could be used for alpha in long/short equities.

## Phase 3: Commercial Impact & STAR (Days 5-6)
- [ ] **Alpha-First Mindset:** Prepare answers focusing on P&L impact, not just AUC/F1 scores.
- [ ] **PM Collaboration:** Practice explaining technical signals in terms of "Market Inefficiency" and "Investment Thesis."
- [ ] **Speed & Scale:** Prepare stories on rapidly prototyping and deploying tools under pressure.

## Phase 4: Final Review (Day 7)
- [ ] **Mock Technical Interview:** Design a system on a whiteboard that analyzes earnings calls for "hidden risks."
- [ ] **Language Check:** Ensure you can explain your technical projects fluently in both English and Mandarin.
- [ ] **Review Hedge Fund News:** Check recent moves in the long/short space or major regulatory shifts in Asian markets.

---
**Key KPIs to Memorize:**
- **Sharpe Ratio:** Risk-adjusted return.
- **Information Coefficient (IC):** Correlation between predicted and actual returns.
- **Drawdown:** Peak-to-trough decline.
- **Turnover:** Frequency of trading.
