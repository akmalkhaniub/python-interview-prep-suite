# Lead Data Scientist (Market Intelligence) - Global Hedge Fund

This module is designed for the Lead Data Scientist role within a Global Hedge Fund's Market Intelligence team. The focus is on deploying AI and agentic workflows to enhance alpha generation for long/short equities, with a critical requirement for Mandarin/English bilingual capability.

## 📁 Module Contents

1.  **[JD Analysis](./jd_analysis.md)**: Deep dive into the "Alpha-first" mindset, PM partnership, and the strategic importance of unstructured data.

3.  **[Preparation Checklist](./checklist.md)**: A 7-day plan focusing on domain mastery, cross-lingual engineering, and commercial impact.

## 🛠️ Key Technologies
- **AI/ML:** LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude, Qwen), Agentic Frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI).
- **Data:** Unstructured data processing (Earnings calls, Filings, Alt Data), Polars, SQL.
- **Finance:** Backtesting, Signal Generation, Risk-adjusted return analysis.
- **Languages:** English and Mandarin (Technical and Stakeholder interaction).

## 📈 Strategic Focus: Alpha Generation
- Augmenting fundamental equity research with AI-driven insights.
- Automating the "discovery" of market inefficiencies.
- High-ownership role with direct P&L impact.
