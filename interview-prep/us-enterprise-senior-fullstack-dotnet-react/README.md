## Role Focus
- **Tech Stack:** .NET, C#, React, TypeScript, SQL Server.
- **Innovation:** Integrating OpenAI/Azure AI/Gemini for intelligent features.
- **Platforms:** Web, Desktop, and Mobile applications.
- **Level:** 6+ years experience with a focus on mentorship and architectural ownership.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **full-stack breadth** (backend, frontend, database, and AI).
- Be prepared to discuss **SQL optimization** and enterprise-scale data design.
- Highlight your experience with **AI integration** and how it adds value to production applications.
