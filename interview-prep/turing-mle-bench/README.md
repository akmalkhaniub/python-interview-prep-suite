## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow, Docker.
- **Concepts:** MLE-Bench, Evaluation Pipelines, Failure Mode Analysis, Performance Optimization.
- **Mission:** Accelerating frontier research by evaluating the capabilities of advanced AI systems.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on the **MLE-Bench style tasks**—understand what it means to evaluate an AI agent's engineering capabilities.
- Be prepared to discuss **navigating large codebases**; you'll be working with existing production-grade repos.
- Highlight your **Python coding standards**; Turing values clean, maintainable, and reproducible code.
- Prepare to discuss **JAX**; it's increasingly used in frontier research labs.
- Emphasize your **Spoken English** and ability to collaborate with SF-based researchers.
