# 7-Day Interview Prep Checklist: MLE Bench (Turing)

## Day 1: ML Fundamentals & Metrics
- [ ] Review **Supervised/Unsupervised Learning** concepts and optimization algorithms.
- [ ] Study **Evaluation Metrics** (Accuracy, F1, NDCG, Perplexity, Pass@k).
- [ ] Research the **MLE-Bench** paper and OpenAI's evaluation methodology.

## Day 2: Advanced PyTorch
- [ ] Review **DDP (Distributed Data Parallel)** and how to debug distributed crashes.
- [ ] Practice writing **Custom Autograd** functions and using **Hooks**.
- [ ] Study **PyTorch Profiler** for identifying performance bottlenecks.

## Day 3: JAX & Functional ML
- [ ] Review **JAX basics**: Pure functions, `jit`, `grad`, `vmap`, and `pmap`.
- [ ] Practice implementing a simple neural network in **JAX**.
- [ ] Study the differences between **JAX and PyTorch** state management.

## Day 4: Production Pipelines & Refactoring
- [ ] Practice **Refactoring** a messy research script into a modular Python package.
- [ ] Review **Docker** for creating reproducible ML environments.
- [ ] Study **Hydra** or **Pydantic** for managing complex ML configurations.

## Day 5: Debugging & Failure Analysis
- [ ] Prepare stories on identifying **Subtle ML Bugs** (e.g., data leakage, gradient vanishing).
- [ ] Practice **Post-Mortem Analysis**: Why would a model fail to optimize on a specific task?
- [ ] Research **Weight Initialization** and **Normalization** strategies for deep networks.

## Day 6: Agentic AI & Frontier Evaluation
- [ ] Review **Chain-of-Thought (CoT)** and **Self-Correction** patterns in LLMs.
- [ ] Study **Multimodal Evaluation** challenges (evaluating vision + code).
- [ ] Research **Sandboxing** and security for running AI agents.

## Day 7: Pitch & English Synchronization
- [ ] Finalize your **MLE success stories**: Complex debugging, pipeline optimization.
- [ ] Prepare your "20+ hrs/week Contractor" pitch: High-autonomy, PST overlap.
- [ ] Mock interview: Explain a **Distributed Training failure** to a researcher in fluent English.
