# Study Plan: Head of AI Systems & Automation (Edge Labs)

This module is designed for a leadership role focused on **Build-First AI Transformation**. The emphasis is on shipping production-ready systems that automate core business functions.

## 🎯 Role Overview
You are the "Execution Lead" for AI. Your mission is to take ambiguous business goals from the CEO and turn them into integrated, automated systems.
1.  **AI Agents & RAG:** Moving beyond "chatbots" to autonomous agents that act on CRM data.
2.  **System Orchestration:** Linking Salesforce, Slack, and Email into a cohesive, intelligent loop.
3.  **Leadership:** Building the team (hiring) and setting the engineering standard for high-velocity AI shipping.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack Focus
*   **LLM Frameworks:** LangChain, LangGraph (for complex stateful agents), Haystack.
*   **Automation Tools:** n8n, Zapier (for rapid prototyping), or custom Python-based orchestration.
*   **Integrations:** Salesforce REST API, Slack Web API, SendGrid/Email protocols.
*   **Data Pipelines:** Vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate) + SQL (PostgreSQL).
