# Head of AI Systems & Automation (Edge Labs)

## About the Role
Edge Labs is the execution engine behind our AI transformation. We are hiring a Head of AI Systems & Automation to lead a small, high-impact team responsible for designing and deploying AI-powered business systems across sales, operations, HR, and compliance. This is a build-first, high-velocity role with a direct CEO mandate.

## Responsibilities
*   **Systems Delivery:** Own end-to-end delivery of AI-powered systems (problem definition to deployment).
*   **Automation:** Build intelligent workflows across Sales, Ops, HR, and Compliance.
*   **Integration:** Design and integrate systems across Salesforce, Slack, email, and internal platforms.
*   **Strategy:** Work directly with the CEO to prioritize and execute high-impact initiatives.
*   **Leadership:** Hire and lead a small team of engineers.
*   **Iterate:** Continuously improve systems based on real-world usage and feedback.

## Requirements
*   **Technical Expertise:** Experience building end-to-end systems combining data, automation, and AI.
*   **Integration:** Strong understanding of APIs, data pipelines, and business systems (CRMs).
*   **Cloud & Language:** Hands-on experience with AWS/GCP and proficiency in Python.
*   **AI Knowledge:** Familiarity with LLMs and AI tools with a focus on practical application.
*   **Execution:** Ability to break down complex business problems into scalable solutions.
*   **Communication:** Strong skills across both technical and business audiences.

## Immediate Priorities
*   Build internal monitoring systems across Slack, email, and CRM.
*   Develop outbound sales and account management automation.
*   Create systems for client health tracking and risk identification.
*   Improve talent pipeline workflows via data-driven matching.
*   Track market signals to prioritize opportunities.
