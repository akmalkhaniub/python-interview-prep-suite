## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Python, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, Redis.
- **Domain:** Automotive (VIN decoding, parts, inventory).
- **Core Skill:** Turning "messy" inconsistent data into reliable datasets at scale.

## Quick Tips
- Emphasize your **Automotive Industry experience**—it's a mandatory requirement.
- Be prepared to discuss **Data Quality** in detail; the JD mentions "messy, incomplete, duplicated data" multiple times.
- Highlight your **problem-solving skills** for complex data flows and "Entity Resolution."
- Mention your experience with **AI/LLMs** for structured data extraction; it's a key part of their future-facing vision.
