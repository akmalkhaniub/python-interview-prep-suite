# Job Description Analysis: Automotive Data Engineer/Scientist

## Role Overview
**Position:** Data Engineer / Data Scientist
**Industry:** Automotive (MANDATORY Experience).
**Core Mission:** Collecting, cleaning, and structuring massive, messy automotive data (VINs, parts, inventory, etc.) into reliable datasets for AI and search.
**Key Differentiator:** Strong focus on "Data Quality" and "Entity Matching" across inconsistent external sources.

## Core Competencies

### 1. Data Engineering & Pipelines
- **ETL/ELT:** Designing scalable pipelines for high-volume data ingestion.
- **Workflow Orchestration:** Managing complex data flows with workers and queues.
- **Data Quality:** Normalization, deduplication, and validation are central to the role.

### 2. Backend & System Architecture
- **Languages:** Python, TypeScript, Go, or Java.
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, and Elasticsearch/OpenSearch.
- **Query Performance:** Designing data models for fast search and analytics.

### 3. AI & Advanced Data Extraction
- **Scraping:** Extracting data from various external web sources.
- **AI-Assisted:** Using AI for data extraction and enrichment.
- **Agentic Workflows:** Supporting future-facing AI automation.

### 4. Automotive Domain Expertise
- **Messy Data:** Handling inconsistent data from dealers, manufacturers, and auctions.
- **Entity Matching:** Correctly identifying the same vehicle or part across different naming conventions.

## Key Interview Themes to Prepare

1.  **Cleaning Messy Automotive Data:** How do you handle incomplete VIN numbers, inconsistent color names, or duplicated inventory listings?
2.  **Entity Resolution at Scale:** Describing your process for "Deduplication" and "Entity Matching" when data comes from 10+ different sources.
3.  **Elasticsearch for Search & Analytics:** How do you index automotive data for sub-second search results?
4.  **Pipeline Monitoring & Alerting:** How do you detect when a scraper fails or when data quality drops below a threshold?
5.  **AI-Assisted Data Extraction:** Discussing how you've used LLMs or AI tools to extract structured data from unstructured automotive descriptions.
6.  **Architectural Trade-offs:** Explaining the trade-offs between "Accuracy" vs. "Speed" when processing millions of rows daily.

## Technical "Must-Haves" for the Interview
- Strong **Python** skills (Pandas, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy).
- Deep knowledge of **PostgreSQL** and **Elasticsearch**.
- Experience with **Workflow Orchestration** (Airflow, Prefect, or Temporal).
- Familiarity with **Automotive data standards** (ACBS, PIES, or general inventory formats).
