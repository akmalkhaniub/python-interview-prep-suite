About the job
GNO Partners: Full-Stack Engineer

GNO Partners helps Amazon sellers run smarter, more profitable businesses. We're building an internal platform that gives our clients a suite of tools to analyze their Amazon operations, surface actionable insights, and push optimizations back to Amazon at scale.

What You'll Do:
Building new report tools end-to-end: from ingesting Amazon reports (CSV/Excel uploads today, APIs soon) to producing bulk operations files.
Owning features across the stack: NestJS APIs, Postgres data models, React frontends, and AWS plumbing.
Working closely with the team to translate Amazon seller workflows into clean, reliable software.
Contributing to the shift toward fully automated data flow with Amazon SP-API and Ads API.
Helping integrate AI-powered insights into reports.

What We're Looking For:
3-5 years of full-stack experience shipping production software.
Strong with TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, and React.
Comfortable with Postgres / Supabase — data modeling and query optimization.
Working knowledge of core AWS services: S3, Lambda, EC2, SNS, SQS.
Basic familiarity with AI agentic systems — LLM-powered features, tool-use, or agent workflows.
Pragmatic, ownership-driven, and comfortable with async communication.

Nice to Have:
Experience with Amazon Seller Central, SP-API, or Ads API.
Background working with large CSV/Excel datasets or report aggregation pipelines.
Prior work on B2B SaaS or analytics platforms.

Offer Details:
Role: Full-time / Remote (Global).
Level: Mid-Level (3-5 years).
Focus: End-to-end Product/Feature development.
