## Role Focus
- **Stack:** TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, React, Postgres, AWS (S3, Lambda, SQS).
- **Concepts:** Report Ingestion, Data Aggregation, Bulk Operations, Agentic AI, Async Communication.
- **Mission:** Helping Amazon sellers run smarter businesses through actionable, automated data insights.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **End-to-End Ownership**—they want engineers who can build features from the database to the UI.
- Be prepared to discuss **CSV/Excel processing**; it's a core part of their current workflow.
- Highlight your **NestJS and Postgres** skills; they are the backbone of the platform.
- Emphasize your **Pragmatism**; GNO Partners values making decisions and shipping impactful work in a small team.
- Mention your curiosity about **Agentic AI**; it's a key part of their roadmap.
