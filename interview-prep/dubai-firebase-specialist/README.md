## Role Focus
- **Stack:** Firebase (Firestore, Functions, Auth, FCM), React.js, PWA.
- **Integrations:** WhatsApp (360dialog), Payments (Tap/Stripe).
- **Market:** UAE (Restaurant/SaaS), Arabic RTL support.

## Quick Tips
- Focus on your **independent building ability**—this JD explicitly says "without hand-holding."
- Be prepared for a **deep dive into Firestore Security Rules**; they mention they will test every "Must-have" skill.
- Highlight any experience with **Arabic RTL** or the **UAE market**; it's a "big advantage."
- Ensure your **GitHub profile** is clean and shows your production Firebase experience clearly.
