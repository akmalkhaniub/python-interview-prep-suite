### About the Role
We are hiring a highly experienced **Anti-Bot Engineer** to design, build, and operate large-scale, resilient web scraping systems that extract high-quality data from heavily protected and constantly evolving web environments.

This is a senior-level, hands-on engineering role focused on building production-grade systems that operate at scale under adversarial conditions (anti-bot systems, dynamic rendering, frequent structural changes, and high-volume data extraction). You will be responsible for architecting and maintaining scraping infrastructure that must remain reliable, observable, and adaptable in real time. The role requires deep technical maturity, strong systems thinking, and the ability to operate independently in complex, ambiguous environments.

This position is best suited to engineers who have already built and operated large-scale distributed systems in production for several years and are comfortable owning mission-critical data pipelines end-to-end.

### Responsibilities
- **Scraping Systems Development:** Design, build, and maintain large-scale Python-based scraping systems targeting highly protected websites (including Google-like environments).
- **Dynamic Rendering:** Architect resilient extraction systems capable of handling dynamic, JavaScript-heavy pages using browser automation and hybrid approaches.
- **System Adaptability:** Continuously adapt systems to frequent changes in page structures, request flows, and anti-bot mechanisms.
- **Data Pipelines:** Build robust, production-grade data extraction pipelines with strong emphasis on accuracy, observability, and fault tolerance.
- **Evasion Strategies:** Implement advanced strategies such as proxy rotation, fingerprinting, session management, and request routing to ensure stability at scale.
- **Production Monitoring:** Monitor system health, proactively detect anomalies, and debug complex production failures across distributed systems.
- **Infrastructure Optimization:** Optimise scraping infrastructure for performance, cost efficiency, and reliability at scale.
- **Collaboration:** Collaborate with data engineering and product teams to ensure scraped data is structured, validated, and trusted.
- **Deployment:** Operate and improve systems running continuously in cloud environments.
- **Documentation:** Document system architecture, scraping logic, and operational procedures for long-term maintainability.

### Requirements
- **Professional Experience:** 7+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a strong focus on backend systems, data engineering, or distributed systems.
- **Web Scraping:** Proven experience building and operating large-scale production web scraping systems.
- **Anti-Bot Navigation:** Deep hands-on experience scraping Google or similarly complex, heavily protected / anti-bot environments.
- **Language Stack:** Strong expertise in **Python** (or comparable production languages such as Go, Rust, or JavaScript).
- **HTTP Internals:** Strong understanding of HTTP internals: headers, cookies, TLS, redirects, sessions, and browser networking behaviour.
- **Automation Frameworks:** Experience with browser automation frameworks such as Playwright, Selenium, Puppeteer, or equivalent.
- **Parsing:** Strong knowledge of HTML parsing, DOM traversal, and high-performance data extraction techniques.
- **Mitigation Handling:** Proven experience handling anti-bot systems, including rate limiting, CAPTCHAs, IP rotation, and fingerprinting.
- **Concurrency:** Experience designing asynchronous and concurrent systems for high-throughput workloads.
- **Debugging:** Strong debugging skills across distributed, failure-prone production systems.
- **Cloud Systems:** Experience running cloud-based systems at scale with strong operational ownership.
