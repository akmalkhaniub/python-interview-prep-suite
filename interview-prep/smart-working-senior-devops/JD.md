# Senior AWS DevOps Engineer (Smart Working)

## About the Role
We’re looking for a Senior AWS DevOps Engineer to join a small, high-impact platform team responsible for building and scaling a cloud-native infrastructure on AWS. This is a hands-on role where you’ll contribute to both the design and implementation of infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and platform tooling.

## Responsibilities
*   **Infrastructure:** Design, build, and maintain AWS infrastructure (EC2, ECS, EKS, Lambda, VPCs).
*   **IaC:** Develop and manage Terraform code for modular, reusable deployments.
*   **CI/CD:** Design and implement GitLab pipelines across application, data, and infrastructure layers.
*   **Kubernetes:** Build and maintain EKS workloads, including deployments and production troubleshooting.
*   **Automation:** Use Packer and Ansible for AMI creation and configuration.
*   **Data Services:** Manage and optimize RDS (PostgreSQL) and ClickHouse.
*   **Observability:** Implement monitoring using CloudWatch, Grafana, Prometheus, and centralized logging (Splunk, Datadog, ELK).
*   **Security:** Apply best practices (IAM, KMS, Secrets Manager).

## Requirements
*   **Experience:** 5+ years in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or SRE roles.
*   **AWS Expertise:** Strong hands-on experience with core AWS services and networking.
*   **CI/CD:** Proven experience with GitLab pipelines.
*   **IaC:** Strong proficiency with Terraform.
*   **Kubernetes:** Hands-on experience with EKS in production.
*   **Automation:** Experience with Packer and Ansible.
*   **Scripting:** Proficiency in Bash, Python, or PowerShell.
*   **Ownership:** Ability to operate in a small, fast-moving team.

## Nice to Have
*   Experience scaling EKS clusters.
*   Multi-account AWS environments and cross-account IAM.
*   Serverless and event-driven architectures (Step Functions, EventBridge).
*   Exposure to specialized pipelines (Unreal Engine workflows, Pixel Streaming).
